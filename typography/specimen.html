Sources and review criteria

Specimen fonts are requested from Google Fonts. Original families: Instrument Sans, Instrument Serif, Geist, Newsreader, Inter, and Playfair Display. No font binaries are bundled. Verify exact files and licenses before distribution.

Check genuine lowercase, real weight and italic files, accents, punctuation, numerals, long copy, fallback legibility, and mobile reflow. Do not use an uppercase transform, small caps, or a caps-only face.
