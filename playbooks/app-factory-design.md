## When design agents enter

After GStack/persona GO, before/during build:

1. Fill `DESIGN-CONTRACT` (template in ops app-factory)  
2. Load SDI skills: `anti-slop-frontend`, `product-ui-polish`, `world-class-web-release`, `motion-and-interaction`  
3. Pick ≤5 pattern IDs from ops `examples/PATTERN-LIBRARY.md`  
4. Use brand pack for this product’s register  
5. Verify with `evals/ui-quality-scorecard.md` + `web-release-gate.md`  

## Style law (short)

- Job hierarchy first  
- shadcn + Lucide + Motion restraint  
- States are product  
- Imagine for heroes, components for chrome  
- Screenshot evidence for ship
