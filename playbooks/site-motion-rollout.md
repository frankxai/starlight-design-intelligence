# Site Motion Rollout

Use this to promote individual motion experiments into a small, governed motion
language.

## Audit

For each candidate route, record the first viewport, mobile state, existing
runtime, reduced-motion behavior, available media, business job, and current
performance. Score the route before selecting work.

## Select

Do not animate the estate. Select:

- one signature proposition-proving moment;
- one product or workflow proof moment;
- one quiet interaction pattern suitable for reuse.

## Specify

Complete `templates/SITE_MOTION_SPEC.md`. Decide what stays still, what moves first,
what the movement proves, the runtime, reduced-motion state, and required evidence.

## Implement by Surface

- **FrankX editorial/relationship:** one artifact-led sequence; no prose animation,
  abstract AI glow, or cinematic delay.
- **FrankX product/technical:** direct workflow trace or before/after proof; animate
  state rather than chrome.
- **Starlight:** agent handoff, evidence stack, evaluation delta, or confidence
  change; avoid hype science fiction.
- **Arcanea:** world-to-product, creator ritual, or symbolic state; never let the
  portal hide the tool.

## Verify and Promote

Inspect desktop, mobile, reduced motion, runtime capture, build checks, and preview.
When the motion passes, extract timing/easing tokens, reusable code, the static
fallback, and before/after evidence. Do not promote a pattern from a description.
