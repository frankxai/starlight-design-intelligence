# Brand Constitution

Use this skill when a brand needs coherence across many agents, repos, products, sites, decks, and launches.

## Typography and kit authority

For Frank's portfolio, apply the standing 9 September 2026 preference across
sites, products, documents, slides, and media: sentence case, real lowercase,
readable body text, and no decorative all caps, small caps, caps-only faces, or
wide-tracked labels. Preserve names, acronyms, code, source text, and existing
wordmark assets.

Use the canonical `starlight-design-intelligence/TYPOGRAPHY.md` and the selected
`brand-packs/<brand>/TYPOGRAPHY.md`. Each kit must name primary, editorial, and
utility roles; exact loaded weights/styles; casing; tracking; sizes; fallback;
font source/rights evidence; and a rendered specimen. Keep colors, logo variants,
image direction, motion, copy, and templates in the same versioned brand pack.

Distinguish a proposed pairing, an existing source declaration, and verified
production use. Preserve brand and surface distinctions. Do not treat a new
font list as a finished brand kit or claim that a saved rule migrated every
consumer. Carry the kit revision into downstream adapters and evidence.

## Constitution Sections

- Promise: what the brand helps people believe or do.
- Audience: who it is for and who it should not chase.
- Voice: sentence shape, confidence level, forbidden language, proof style.
- Visual identity: color, type, layout, imagery, density, iconography.
- Motion identity: timing, motifs, reduced-motion policy, product vs marketing behavior.
- Product behavior: how interfaces feel, respond, explain, and recover.
- Content system: recurring formats, calls to action, proof assets, examples.
- Guardrails: what would make the brand feel generic, false, or off-world.

## Output

Return a compact constitution with concrete do/don't rules and implementation notes.
