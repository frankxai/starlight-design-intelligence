# Brand Constitution

Use this skill when a brand needs coherence across many agents, repos, products, sites, decks, and launches.

## Constitution Sections

- Promise: what the brand helps people believe or do.
- Audience: who it is for and who it should not chase.
- Voice: sentence shape, confidence level, forbidden language, proof style.
- Visual identity: color, type, layout, imagery, density, iconography.
- Motion identity: timing, motifs, reduced-motion policy, product vs marketing behavior.
- Product behavior: how interfaces feel, respond, explain, and recover.
- Content system: recurring formats, calls to action, proof assets, examples.
- Guardrails: what would make the brand feel generic, false, or off-world.

## Output

Return a compact constitution with concrete do/don't rules and implementation notes.
