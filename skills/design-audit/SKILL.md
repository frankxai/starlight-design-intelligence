# Design Audit

Use this skill for a senior, direct design critique.

## Score

Rate 1-5 across: purpose, hierarchy, layout, type, color, density, imagery, interaction, motion, accessibility, brand fit, and production readiness.

## Findings

Lead with the highest-risk issues. Prefer specific fixes over aesthetic commentary.

## Output

Return: overall score, top risks, high-leverage fixes, brand-fit notes, motion/accessibility notes, and acceptance criteria.
