# Editorial Articulation Gate

Score each item 0, 1, or 2. Release still requires at least **18/20** and no automatic failure. This is only the diagnostic floor, not evidence of exceptional craft, audience preference, or release readiness by itself. Apply the selected brand pack; FrankX is not the default voice for the portfolio.

| Item | Passing evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient reality | Exact person or buying moment is legible |
| Observed truth | A real moment, artifact, behavior, quote, or result |
| Proposition | Smallest credible next move is explicit |
| First 120 words | Why, observation, proposition, and reason to continue |
| Sentence craft | Human cadence, concrete nouns, active verbs |
| Taxonomy restraint | Builder language follows value and proof |
| Proof adjacency | Evidence sits next to each important claim |
| Voice | The selected brand's speaker, worldview, and sentence rhythm are recognizable |
| Deletion discipline | No grand line survives without carrying meaning |
| Independent read | Non-author identifies recipient and proposition precisely |

Automatic failure: invented quotation or endorsement, simulated intimacy,
recipient-name-swappable praise, architecture before relationship, false
inevitability, or a CTA disguising a sales script as generosity.

## Distinction review

An independent reader reviews the actual wording in its intended composition. Record the original draft, revision, reader observations, and unresolved disagreement. Do not invent reader results or convert an author's judgment into audience evidence.

1. **Point of view:** What does this brand believe that changes the work? Identify one meaningful choice or exclusion. An adjective is not a position.
2. **Substitution:** Swap the brand with another portfolio brand. Which sentence, specimen, or interaction becomes wrong? If nothing changes, revisit the premise.
3. **Human stakes:** Why would this particular person care today? Name their situation without requiring internal taxonomy.
4. **Memorable substance:** After one reading, what idea or detail remains? A retained slogan without comprehension is incomplete.
5. **Craft:** Does the rhythm serve the meaning? Read it aloud; remove stock cadences, repetitive fragments, decorative contrasts, and claims that become weaker under scrutiny.
6. **Behavior:** Does the next action deliver what the language leads the person to expect? Inspect the actual path, including unavailable and failure states.

For a flagship proposition, seek observations from intended readers as well as the independent editorial reviewer. Ask them to describe the product, expected result, distinguishing idea, and next step in their own words. These observations are qualitative; record participant count and context without claiming statistical superiority.

## Wording across the experience

Review discovery, hero, supporting explanation, navigation, onboarding, work states, offers, error recovery, and follow-up together. Each has a different job; they must agree on product truth.

- An aspiration may be expansive. An availability, price, compatibility, or outcome claim needs current evidence.
- “Saved,” “approved,” “exported,” and “published” refer to verified states, not button clicks.
- A failure message names what happened, what is preserved, and the available next action. Claim preservation only when it is confirmed.
- Mythic language can be appropriate in Arcanea story and canon. Essential product instructions remain comprehensible without lore knowledge.
- A word scan catches mechanical problems; it cannot decide whether a piece has taste, truth, or originality.

Exceptional work must satisfy the ordinary gates and survive this deeper read. If a verdict is only “clean,” “professional,” or “on brand,” identify the missing authored decision before calling it distinctive.
