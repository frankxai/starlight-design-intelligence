# Anti-Slop Checklist

Use this before publishing generated UI.

- The first viewport shows the real product, offer, workflow, or object.
- The page does not rely on nested cards, generic gradients, or decorative blobs.
- Typography scale matches the surface: product UI is compact; heroes are reserved for true heroes.
- Buttons and controls use familiar affordances.
- Colors do not collapse into one hue family.
- Imagery is meaningful and inspectable.
- Motion clarifies state, hierarchy, or story.
- Reduced-motion behavior exists for non-essential movement.
- Mobile and desktop have no text overlap or clipped controls.
- Claims are supported by proof, screenshots, demos, or mechanism.
