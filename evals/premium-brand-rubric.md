# Premium Brand Rubric

Score each area 1-5.

| Area | 1 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distinctiveness | Could be any AI site | Feels ownable and memorable |
| Clarity | Promise is vague | Promise and next step are obvious |
| Visual system | Random styles | Coherent type, color, space, imagery |
| Motion | Decorative or absent | Purposeful, branded, accessible |
| Proof | Claims float | Evidence appears near claims |
| Brand boundary | Tone leaks across brands | FrankX, Starlight, Arcanea stay distinct |
| Production readiness | Pretty mock only | Responsive, accessible, implementable |

Scores under 25 need another pass before publication.
