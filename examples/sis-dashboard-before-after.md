# SIS Dashboard Before / After

## Weak Before

- Oversized marketing hero inside an operational dashboard.
- Cards compete for attention without ownership, status, or next action.
- Activity is decorative rather than tied to agent work.
- Alerts and evaluation results are separated from evidence.

## Strong After

- Stable command shell with compact navigation, queue, alerts, and current system state.
- Each panel answers: what is happening, who owns it, what changed, what needs action.
- Eval deltas animate subtly when scores change.
- Agent handoffs and escalations are visible as traces, not mystery motion.
- Evidence, logs, and references sit near decisions.

## Acceptance

An operator can scan state, identify risk, and choose the next action without reading a paragraph.
