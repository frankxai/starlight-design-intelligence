# Arcanea Academy Before / After

## Weak Before

- Generic course grid with purple glow and fantasy words.
- Lore hides what the learner actually does.
- Cards feel interchangeable.
- Motion is decorative sparkle.

## Strong After

- The path feels like initiation, but every step has a clear action and outcome.
- Lessons are artifacts with visible progress, prerequisites, and finished outputs.
- Glyph or portal motion reveals relationships between skills, worlds, and creative deliverables.
- The interface keeps controls plain while the atmosphere carries the mythic layer.

## Acceptance

The experience feels enchanted and premium, while the learner always knows the next concrete step.
