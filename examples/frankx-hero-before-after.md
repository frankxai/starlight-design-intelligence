# FrankX Hero Before / After

## Weak Before

- Headline says "Unlock AI-powered growth" without naming the offer.
- Hero uses abstract gradients and no product proof.
- CTA says "Get started" with no expectation of what happens next.
- Supporting copy makes broad claims without mechanism.

## Strong After

- Headline names the offer: "Build an agentic income system you can actually operate."
- First viewport shows a real system map, repo/plugin stack, or dashboard screenshot.
- CTA says "Run the income-system audit" or "Install the starter skill pack."
- Supporting copy names the mechanism: skills, plugins, checkout, content loop, verification.
- Motion is direct: one short reveal of the system map or proof trail, no decorative loops.

## Acceptance

The user should know the promise, proof, and next action within five seconds.
