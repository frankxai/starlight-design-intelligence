# Observatory Governance

## Authority

`frankxai/starlight-design-intelligence` is the only canonical design authority for the portfolio. Reference websites do not receive repositories. Their evidence is separated by stable `target_id` inside this observatory.

`visual-intelligence` remains narrowly responsible for visual-asset inventory, deduplication, provenance checks, quality assurance, and publishing gates. It does not own brand rules, competitive research, patterns, or domain direction.

The private `ux-design` repository contains no unique design intelligence. Its unique material is a proprietary/confidential license record and a Playwright MCP bootstrap. Preserve its history as a read-only historical tooling capsule; do not delete it or promote it into a second authority.

## Public and private boundary

Public Git may contain:

- intentionally published, portfolio-owned brand contracts;
- normalized observed facts and clearly labeled inferences;
- content hashes, content addresses, tool metadata, and rights classifications;
- rights-safe abstract pattern cards and small deterministic diagrams;
- domain direction records that contain owned product truth.

Private R2 must contain:

- raw third-party screenshots and HTML;
- crawler bundles and interaction recordings;
- licensed source packages or confidential product evidence.

Proprietary brand packs may remain public only when Frank intentionally publishes them as an owned operating contract. Unreleased strategy, secrets, customer material, credentials, and raw competitive evidence remain private. Publication never changes third-party rights.

## Rights

All first-wave third-party material is `third-party-reference-only` unless a reviewed license explicitly says otherwise. Production use is limited to original recomposition of an abstract principle. Never copy marks, assets, font files, source code, components, distinctive compositions, language, or protected trade dress.

## Decision integrity

Every active domain maps to exactly one canonical brand pack. Each domain keeps exactly three materially different directions, one nomination, source-to-principle influence, anti-references, and forbidden blending. Rejected directions are preserved until Frank decides; they are never silently merged.

## Promotion

A pattern may be `approved` only when its abstract mechanism is supported by recorded observations and passes the rights boundary. Responsive and motion guidance requires exact viewport, interaction, and reduced-motion evidence. A domain nomination is not production approval.
