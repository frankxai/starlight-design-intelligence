# Shared editorial standard

Public copy should leave the reader clear about the subject, evidence, decision, and next action. Each brand supplies its own rhythm and vocabulary.

## Hard failures

- Rhetorical contrast formulas such as “X, not Y,” “this isn't X, it's Y,” “not just X,” and “more than just X.” Literal negation used for accuracy remains valid.
- Generic marketing frames such as “unlock the power,” “where X meets Y,” “take it to the next level,” and “the possibilities are endless.”
- Unsupported claims about leadership, quality, results, urgency, partnerships, customers, first-person experience, or product readiness.
- Abstract labels that hide an ordinary fact or action.
- Slash-separated prestige labels without a real classification purpose.
- All-caps public prose outside approved acronyms, marks, and compact data labels.
- Meta-writing openers, praise, engagement bait, and generic conclusions.
- Fabricated facts, quotations, citations, endorsements, or intimacy.

## Review signals

- Repeated abstract nouns.
- Formulaic groups of three.
- Uniform paragraph rhythm.
- Stacked formal transitions.
- Dramatic fragment sequences.
- Excessive em dashes.
- Generic adjectives without adjacent evidence.
- Internal taxonomy appearing before reader value.
- Search terms repeated at the cost of natural language.

Signals require judgment. Technical terms such as optimize, robust, verify, align, and leverage remain valid when they carry their exact meaning. Arcanea may use words such as realm, ritual, and journey when the scene or product gives them substance.

## Truth and evidence

- Preserve exact quotations, code, legal wording, data, approved product terms, and necessary technical language.
- Date version-sensitive claims.
- Put proof beside the claim it supports.
- Label shipped capability, active experiment, proposal, and aspiration accurately.
- Disclose affiliate relationships near commercial recommendations.

## Final read

Read the copy aloud. Remove any line that sounds engineered to impress. A separate editor or deterministic audit must review generated public copy before release.
