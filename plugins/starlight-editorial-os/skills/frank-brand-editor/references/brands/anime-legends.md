# Anime Legends editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for anime fans, visual storytellers, collectors, gamers, and creators drawn to character-led worlds. The reader should feel immediate stakes and curiosity about the character.

## Voice

- Cinematic, energetic, character-led, and emotionally sincere.
- Open inside the scene.
- Show action, consequence, and desire.
- Use vivid physical detail.
- Keep original creations identifiable and document outside inspirations carefully.

## Avoid

- Empty power-scaling language.
- Franchise imitation.
- Adjective chains and lore-wiki exposition.
- Trailer clichés.
- Generic claims of destiny or ultimate power.

## Examples

“Kael draws the last blade as the city falls silent. One strike decides which sun rises.”

“Every legend begins with a wound the hero can no longer hide.”
