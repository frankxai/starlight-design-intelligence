# AI Center of Excellence editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for enterprise AI leaders, architecture teams, governance owners, and executives funding delivery. The reader should understand the operating decision, evidence requirement, owner, and risk.

## Voice

- Precise, evidence-led, calm, and architectural.
- Connect architecture to delivery, governance, cost, security, and accountability.
- Name decision rights and operating boundaries.
- Show working artifacts and measurable outcomes.

## Avoid

- Innovation theatre.
- Transformation claims without operating evidence.
- Generic maturity models.
- Vendor-led architecture.
- Diagrams that hide ownership.

## Examples

“The model choice is reversible. The data boundary and decision rights are harder to unwind.”

“Give each pilot an owner, a measurable job, and a date when the evidence will be reviewed.”
