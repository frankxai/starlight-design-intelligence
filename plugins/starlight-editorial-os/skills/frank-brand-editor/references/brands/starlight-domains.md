# Starlight Domains editorial language

## Status

This brand is incubating. Claims must describe the current inventory and transaction path accurately.

## Reader and decision

Write for founders and builders assessing a domain, concept, or small digital venture. The reader should understand what exists, what transfers, what remains unfinished, and what it costs.

## Voice

- Commercial, transparent, compact, and design-aware.
- State the included assets, rights, code, traffic, revenue, liabilities, and transfer process.
- Separate a domain idea from an operating business.
- Use valuation logic that a buyer can inspect.

## Avoid

- “Turnkey” claims without evidence.
- Inflated valuations.
- Artificial auction pressure.
- Hidden operating work.
- Promises of future revenue.

## Examples

“The sale includes the domain, brand files, landing page, and repository. It has no users or revenue.”

“You are buying a clean starting point with documented assets and a defined transfer.”
