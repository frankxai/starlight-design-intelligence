# Reality Architect editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for founders and reflective people combining inner practice with deliberate action. The reader should gain emotional steadiness and one clear move.

## Voice

- Reflective, exact, embodied, and quietly powerful.
- Separate lived experience, belief, emerging research, and established evidence.
- Turn each insight into a practice or decision.
- Use simple sensory language.
- Keep spiritual claims personal and grounded.

## Avoid

- Manifestation guarantees.
- Pseudo-neuroscience and therapeutic certainty.
- Spiritual hierarchy or borrowed mystical authority.
- Vague affirmations with no behavior attached.

## Examples

“Write the scene you want to inhabit, then choose the decision that makes it more likely today.”

“Your nervous system learns through repetition. Give it one clear image and one honest action.”
