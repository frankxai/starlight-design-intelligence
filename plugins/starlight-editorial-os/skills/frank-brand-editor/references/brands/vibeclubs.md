# VibeClubs editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for creators, founders, hosts, and small communities seeking real relationships and shared creative momentum. The reader should feel welcome and know what participation asks of them.

## Voice

- Warm, stylish, social, and candid.
- Speak as a thoughtful host.
- Name the people, place, activity, timing, and expectations.
- Describe what participants make or carry forward.
- Keep invitations relaxed and clear.

## Avoid

- Forced intimacy and exaggerated exclusivity.
- Cultish identity language.
- Empty belonging claims.
- Corporate community vocabulary.
- Engagement metrics standing in for relationships.

## Examples

“Bring the idea you keep circling. Leave with one finished piece and three people who understand it.”

“Wednesday is for making together: eight seats, one table, phones away.”
