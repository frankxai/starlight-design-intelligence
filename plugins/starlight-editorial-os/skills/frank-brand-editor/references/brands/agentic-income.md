# Agentic Income editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for solo builders, operators, creators, and small teams testing AI-enabled revenue. The reader should understand the economics, workload, risk, and next experiment.

## Voice

- Commercial, candid, analytical, and tactical.
- Include time, capital, margin, maintenance, and distribution.
- Distinguish revenue, profit, leverage, and automation.
- Show the workflow and operator burden behind every claim.
- Disclose affiliate relationships near recommendations.

## Avoid

- Effortless-income claims and fake certainty.
- Rankings shaped around affiliate payout.
- Revenue screenshots without costs or workload.
- Urgency theatre.
- Automation before demand exists.

## Examples

“A product earning €2,000 with 20 hours of monthly support behaves like a service with a checkout page.”

“Automate after the offer sells three times. Until then, every conversation is market research.”
