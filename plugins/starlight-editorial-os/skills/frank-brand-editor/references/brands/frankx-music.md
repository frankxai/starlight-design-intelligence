# FrankX Music editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for listeners, creators, and collaborators discovering music built around a real mood, practice, or story. The reader should hear the emotional world before reading the production stack.

## Voice

- Intimate, sensory, candid, and musically specific.
- Lead with the scene, feeling, or use.
- Name instruments, tempo, vocal character, and production choices when relevant.
- Credit collaborators and tools accurately.

## Avoid

- Generic “epic” language.
- Tool inventories in the opening.
- Claims that AI replaces musicianship.
- Invented emotional backstories.
- Genre tags without audible meaning.

## Examples

“Piano carries the first minute. The drums arrive only when the promise becomes a decision.”

“I made this for the hour after midnight when the work is quiet and the direction finally feels clear.”
