# Brand experience protocol

Status: proposed extension. Current product decisions govern proposition and availability; approved brand references govern identity. Record conflicts explicitly when older pack copy describes a superseded product model. This protocol does not revise those source decisions.

A brand should help a person anticipate a useful experience and recognise it when it happens. Define what the person will do, the evidence that it worked, and the cue that lets them remember the brand at the next relevant moment.

## The ambition must change the work

Meeting a release checklist establishes a floor. Distinction comes from a point of view that changes what the brand makes, what it refuses, how it speaks, and what people experience repeatedly.

Keep four levels explicit:

1. **Cultural ambition:** the contribution the brand intends to make over years.
2. **Point of view:** a belief that leads to meaningful choices and exclusions.
3. **Present promise:** the useful thing this version can deliver to this person.
4. **Signature experience:** the recurring act or authored work through which people recognize that promise.

A larger ambition does not require a larger first product. GenCreator can pursue generative culture through the precise act of helping a creator give a source a distinct, approved expression. The quality of that act earns permission for a wider cultural role.

## Distinct centers of gravity

These are proposed strategic expressions for the existing packs, not a renaming of brands, businesses, or products. Each [brand pack](../brand-packs/) contains the audience, language, signature work, and product consequences.

| Brand | What people should come to it for | Signature evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FrankX | Judgment about what to build and why | A consequential decision and inspectable work |
| GenCreator | Authored expression in generative culture | A real source, the creator's choices, a finished expression |
| Arcanea | A creative world worth continuing | A creation developed within remembered context |
| Starlight Intelligence | A future they can meaningfully contribute to | A frontier linked to research, learning, or practical contribution |
| Vibeclubs | Shared presence and creative belonging | A hosted gathering with a real participant contribution |
| AnimeLegends | Original characters and stories worth following | A released scene with a choice and consequence |
| Starlight Technology | A considered complete-system purchase | A workload, compatible setup, dated evidence, and tradeoffs |
| AI Architect Academy / CoE | Architecture judgment and organizational capability | A design decision tested against a changed scenario |
| Reality Architect | Understanding and changing a recurring system | A map, a bounded intervention, and an observed result |
| Rial / Aurevia Energy | An understandable local solar decision | A complete proposal with available facts and human review |
| Agentic Income | A service whose delivery and economics can be inspected | A fulfilled outcome with real costs and intervention points |

Do not force every brand to promise productivity, community, or intelligence. Shared engineering should make the portfolio reliable; the brands should remain different reasons to care.

## Writing that carries a point of view

Start with the person's stakes and the brand's judgment. Then explain what can happen here. Technical nouns help only where they sharpen understanding.

| Habit to remove | More useful direction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| “The operating system for AI-native creators” as the whole proposition | “Generative culture. With a point of view.” followed by a concrete source-to-work demonstration | Gives GenCreator an authored cultural ambition and a product reason to believe |
| “Unlock limitless creativity” | “Begin with a character. Give them a world.” | Gives Arcanea a concrete creative doorway |
| “Join our thriving ecosystem” | Name the next gathering, its host, and what a participant brings | Makes Vibeclubs an experience a person can choose |
| “Next-generation intelligence infrastructure” at first contact | Name a Starlight frontier and the contribution a person can make | Connects institutional ambition to human participation |
| “Premium AI solutions” | Show the FrankX decision, working example, and conditions in which it applies | Lets judgment establish authority |

A memorable line is not required to explain the entire product. The surrounding composition must do that work. Copy should hold attention through specificity, rhythm, contrast of scale, and an earned surprise. Avoid making every sentence a slogan or every paragraph a three-part manifesto.

Apple and other admired brands are craft references, not templates or borrowed authority. Study how a reference directs attention and resolves a decision. Produce original language and evidence suitable to this brand; do not claim superiority without a defined, measured comparison.

## Apply the direction to important files

Work outward from authority so improved wording survives the next agent session:

| File or surface | Required change |
|---|---|
| Brand pack | Define ambition, audience tension, point of view, voice, signature work, and exclusions |
| Product decision | State the current customer, useful mechanism, availability, and proof gap |
| AGENTS / CLAUDE | Route to the current brand and product sources; remove superseded promises |
| Local design / taste contract | Translate the brand into composition, interaction, imagery, and editorial behavior while preserving approved tokens |
| Homepage, about, navigation | Give each surface a distinct job and a comprehensible first action |
| Product states and errors | Tell the truth about saving, reviewing, approving, exporting, payment, and recovery |
| Metadata, structured data, README, social preview | Describe the same current product, adapted to discovery rather than copied from the hero |
| Onboarding, offers, email, community | Continue the same promise through a real outcome; name the owner of each handoff |
| Tests, CI, release evidence | Prove the behavior that makes the words true |

Audit these as a connected path. Fixing a hero while the README teaches an obsolete product creates drift. A corrected strategy is a proposal until adopted; record downstream adoption separately from authoring.

## Build recognition through work

Begin with one flagship specimen per active brand, then adapt it to the places where that audience already makes relevant decisions. GenCreator can begin with an authored work and its source study; Arcanea with a creation and its world context; FrankX with a decision and build notes.

Carry the approved mark, distinctive composition, verbal perspective, and relevant source through the derivatives. Adapt the form to the place: a short film must work as a film, a product page must enable a decision, and a README must help someone use the work. Repeating a hero sentence everywhere is not an editorial system.

Expand distribution when the flagship work communicates the intended meaning and the product delivers the implied promise. Track recognition and meaningful use separately. Do not call a new series a cultural movement before people voluntarily make it one.

## Required brief

Every active product or campaign brief names:
- A stable brand ID and the existing brand-pack path.
- A product ID, owning repository, surface and accountable owner.
- A concrete audience situation and one current, deliverable promise.
- A voluntary role the task helps the person practise.
- Existing visual, verbal or sonic cue references and their approval state.
- The smallest useful action and its observable completion event.
- A real specimen, source and proof limit.
- The next relevant action after value, including any cross-brand handoff.
- A measurement plan, reviewer, version and decision status.

The [brief template](../examples/brand-experience-brief.json) is planning data. It is not a runtime schema, proof of adoption or permission to change a public identity. Never use a generated brief to override the immutable design contract.

## Experience sequence

Use a recognizable cue near a credible promise. Let the user do a small piece of the actual work. Confirm the result accurately and give them control over saving, sharing or continuing it. Keep the cue associated with that result across approved formats.

For FrankX this can be an architecture decision made explicit. For GenCreator it can be a source-linked draft that the creator reviews and exports. For Arcanea it can be a connected world seed that remains coherent as the creator develops it. Each uses its own brand pack.

Use accurate states: drafted, reviewed, exported and published mean different things. A click on an export control is not proof of a successful export. A static demo must be labelled as a demo.

## Portfolio boundaries

One product has one owning brand, even when distributed through several sites or marketplaces. A brand, domain, legal owner, product, artist, imprint and community are separate entities.

Cross-brand descriptions name the visitor's task. State technical dependencies only when they exist. Endorsement language must distinguish authorship, portfolio membership, technical integration and external endorsement.

Reuse stable brand cues without imposing shared fonts, colors or motion on the entire portfolio. Preserve each pack's surface modes. An identity exploration is not an approved mark.

## Distribution

Adapt one source-backed product story into relevant formats: a demonstration, an annotated specimen, a lesson, a product listing and a useful follow-up. Each derivative retains the product version, proof source, availability and owner.

Books and workbooks should make the brand's practice tangible. Templates should retain an inspectable worked example. Software confirms actual completion. Media should show the work at the moment the brand cue appears. Gatherings create a participant contribution.

Maintain recognition while varying the stories. Repetition of the same unsupported claim does not create proof.

## Evidence and measurement

Keep experimental priming results separate from product promises. The [Apple/IBM study](https://doi.org/10.1086/527269) does not establish an uplift for another brand. A [preregistered stereotype-susceptibility replication](https://doi.org/10.1027/1864-9335/a000193) did not reproduce the original performance effect. No demographic stereotype is needed to invite someone into useful work.

Use [category entry points](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/identifying-and-prioritising-category-entry-points) to define the situations in which people should remember the brand. Test comprehension, correct attribution, completed work and meaningful return separately.

Record a baseline before claiming lift. An experiment declares one primary outcome, assignment unit, denominator, observation window and stop rule. Small task-observation sessions guide design; they do not establish population effect sizes.

Use the product's existing analytics and consent mechanisms. Event properties may include brand_id, product_id, surface_id, experiment_id, variant, asset_version and proof_id. Raw user content and sensitive identity inferences do not belong in events.

## Review

A reviewer must be able to identify the audience situation, the task, the evidence and the next action without reading the strategy.

Reject a brief when the promise is unavailable, the proposed identity relies on superiority over other people, proof is fabricated, the cue has no approved source, or the brand could be swapped without changing the task.

Public interface changes still follow the existing web release gate. This protocol adds a product-experience question to planning; it does not create a new deployment approval or bypass an existing one.
