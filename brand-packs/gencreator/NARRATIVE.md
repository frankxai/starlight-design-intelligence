# An edition with an author

Apply [honor and responsibility](../../playbooks/honor-and-responsibility.md) within the existing [brand](BRAND.md) and [source authority](SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md). The approved paper, ink and red territory remains authoritative; this adds no identity or wordmark decision.

**Reader and job:** An expert-led creator wants to turn source material into an authored edition they can stand behind.

**Promise:** Make the source, the human decisions and the finished edition legible.

**Behavior:** Preserve the source and credit, distinguish a generated draft from human review, show meaningful corrections, and let the creator inspect the exact exported object. Saved, reviewed, exported and published are different states.

**Tension:** Faster production is useful only when the creator still recognizes the work as their own. A convincing edition preview can conceal missing persistence or an export that loses attribution.

**Proof:** A real source-to-draft-to-correction-to-edition sequence; reopening demonstrates persistence, and opening the actual export demonstrates its content and credit. A design territory or approved preview proves neither action.

## Copy specimen

> Your source is the beginning. Your choices make the edition.
>
> Review the draft against the original. Keep the passages you can stand behind, correct what misses your meaning, and check the credit before exporting this edition.

Attach this to an actual review flow. Where saving or exporting is unavailable, label the demo and its limits. Do not use “your edition is saved” until reopening the saved revision succeeds.

## Edition contracts

- Source input retains provenance and applicable permission. Private notes do not become public examples without authorization.
- Human review records a deliberate change or acceptance; generating text is not the creator's approval.
- Revisions preserve the original source relationship. An edit to attribution updates the exported object's credit too.
- Export presents the format and actual content, retains the reviewed edition on failure and marks exported only after the file exists.
- Publication requires a separate release decision. Community critique supports authorship with a precise question about an artifact, not endless content volume.

The name-swap distinction is an authored edition: source, editorial choice, signed review state and inspected export. Keep it distinct from FrankX teaching, Starlight mission coordination and automated publishing claims.
