# GenCreator brand

GenCreator serves expert-led creators who want to shape their knowledge into
authored work while retaining source evidence, judgment and control of release.
The shared identity follows the product owner's accepted Territory B decision:
new creative publishing. See [source authority](SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md).

## Promise and proof

Turn what you know into what only you can make. The identity expresses a source,
conversation, connected artifacts, human review and a signed edition. Each surface
must show which parts of that workflow actually work at its current revision.

The product is the creation and review layer. Community is the practice and peer
review layer, organised around an actual artifact and a precise critique request.
Research, member material and services support that work; a catalog or cohort
ladder does not define the brand.

## Boundaries

- Preserve authorship, source links and explicit review states.
- Show real artifacts and verified current availability. Saved, approved,
  exported and published require evidence of those respective actions.
- Keep unavailable products, proposed pricing and future integrations accurately
  labelled. An edition metaphor does not prove a publishing capability.
- Territory approval covers the visual direction. Wordmark selection, legal
  screening, application proofs and production rollout remain pending.
