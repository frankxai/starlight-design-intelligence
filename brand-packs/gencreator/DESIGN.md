# GenCreator Design

Read [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) for the cultural ambition: generative culture with a point of view.

## Governing composition

Make authored work the visual subject. The strongest composition connects a source, a consequential creative choice, and its resulting expression. Product control should be legible around that work without becoming the entire identity.

A creator should recognize their contribution before noticing the machinery. Preserve intentional language and meaningful visual differences between creators. Do not make every output resemble a GenCreator advertisement.

## Product and editorial modes

Product surfaces serve concentration: an artifact, relevant source context, review controls, and one clear next action. Keep technical detail available where it helps inspect the result.

Editorial and cultural surfaces can breathe. Give a finished piece scale, credit its author, and explain one choice that shaped it. Use original work, an annotated edit, or a creator's specific observation. A stock desk does not establish creative culture.

Community surfaces make work and critique visible. A contribution should have an author, context, and a clear question or invitation. Avoid anonymous output feeds.

## Identity and adoption

Keep the approved mark, typography, color, and motion sources pinned by the consuming repository's design contract. This revision removes the earlier warm-paper / fourteen-product-catalog prescription; it does not select new visual tokens.

For GenCreator product implementation, reconcile the pinned pack with the owning repository's `design.md` and `taste.md` in a reviewed adoption change. That change must bind an immutable kernel revision and include rendered evidence. Do not silently repin a consumer or reinterpret an old token source as a new identity.

## Authored detail

- Show source excerpts at a readable scale, with context and permitted attribution.
- Give the artifact enough space to judge its writing or composition.
- Use annotation to reveal a choice; do not cover the work in status badges.
- Reserve motion for a real relationship or state change, such as comparing an edit or confirming an approval.
- Keep the complete composition understandable with reduced motion.
- A preview must look like an honest preview, with its limitations where the person makes a decision.

## Release question

Can someone identify the creator's point of view, understand what GenCreator contributed, and take the next action without learning the internal architecture? Verify that on the actual desktop and mobile result.
