# GenCreator design

## Approved direction

Territory B, new creative publishing, is documented as accepted by Frank on
2026-08-22. Its source, scope and remaining gates are recorded in
[source authority](SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md). This pack corrects the later shared green
palette; it does not select a logo or approve a new application.

- Default to light paper `#fbf9f5` and ink `#221f1b`. They carry at least 90% of
  a composition. Spot red `#c8322a` appears once per object to register an edition,
  source, state or signature. Use a separate accessible semantic treatment when
  the actual state needs it; brand red alone must not communicate meaning.
- Instrument Serif carries releases and editorial display. Instrument Sans carries
  reading, product controls and evidence. Both source fonts have OFL 1.1 licenses
  in the owning product's exploration record. Verify actual deployed files,
  rendered families, weights and mobile specimens before release.
- Use sentence case. Reserve mono for source IDs, hashes and timestamps.
- Compose one dominant editorial move through scale, sequence, measure and a
  restrained hairline grid. Edition numbers, source notes and a visible review
  state should belong to the work they identify.
- Commission imagery for the specific release: practice, tools, working material
  or source fragments. Generated work needs the required provenance and human
  review. A raster concept is never a vector master.
- Motion can gather source fragments, reveal a fold, resolve a relationship or
  confirm the creator's decision. Reduced motion must preserve the same evidence
  and states. Avoid perpetual orbit, ambient glow and generic reveal sequences.

## Identity and migration

There is no approved production wordmark in the v3 source. Gather, Signature and
Continuum are candidates for human selection. The old blue/cyan/green loop-arrow
remains a transitional runtime asset in the product; do not promote it as the
future identity or overwrite it with a generated sketch. New internal documents
can use clean text, `GenCreator`, in Instrument Sans Medium.

The product's migration is surface by surface through `data-territory="b"`.
Preserve existing routes and pending work. `product-intelligence` and
`community-editorial` are adoption mode IDs, not color territories or approval
receipts. Community can use its own practical layout and social tone within the
shared authorship identity. Its current local palette is not a new founder-approved
territory; reconcile a palette change through its owner and application QA.

Correcting this pack does not update a downstream SHA pin, change a page, populate
Figma or close a release gate. The Territory B application quartet, image-world
benchmark and product-family scale approval remain pending.
