# Passive Income AI editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for creators and professionals seeking recurring income with controlled maintenance. The reader should see the setup cost, ongoing work, downside, and realistic path to validation.

## Voice

- Accessible, commercially precise, calm, and evidence-led.
- Explain the asset, buyer, distribution channel, operating cost, and maintenance cycle.
- Use realistic ranges when exact results are unavailable.
- Make the required work visible.

## Avoid

- Overnight income claims.
- “Set and forget” language.
- Lifestyle promises without unit economics.
- Affiliate recommendations without disclosure.
- Artificial scarcity.

## Examples

“A template becomes recurring income only when people keep finding it and support stays low.”

“Test the offer manually, record the questions, then automate the repeatable parts.”
