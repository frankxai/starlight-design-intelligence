# Rial / Aurevia Energy Brand

Rial Energy is the canonical parent pack for PV Lager, Aurevia Solar, and Zola Car Pod customer surfaces.

## Promise

Turn a complex solar purchase into an inventory-aware, subsidy-aware, complete-system recommendation with a human review path.

## Position

Make a substantial local purchase understandable. Trust comes from knowing what is available, what fits the site, which assumptions affect the economics, and who will answer when a question cannot be settled online.

The parent pack provides that discipline. PV Lager emphasizes available components and complete orders; Aurevia Solar emphasizes considered system planning; Zola Car Pod explains its specific product and fit. Confirm each live offer before applying these expressions to a sales surface.

## Language and proof

Proposed service line: **A solar plan you can understand. A team you can reach.** Use the second sentence only beside a maintained human contact path.

Write in the customer's language and local context. Translate technical units into the decision they affect while retaining exact specifications where needed. “Check your system requirements” is appropriate before a qualified recommendation; “See available components” requires current inventory.

Show the system, what is included, what still needs site assessment, dated assumptions, and the responsible next step. Images should help a customer understand installation, dimensions, or compatibility. Attractive panels alone do not answer the buying question.

## Product consequence

An estimate remains identifiable as an estimate through the inquiry and quote. Inventory changes must not silently become delivery promises. A recommendation should preserve its inputs so a human can review it. Assess clarity, qualified inquiries, and avoidable corrections alongside sales.

## Boundaries

- Preserve local Seesen trust and distinguish stocked facts from modeled estimates.
- Real customer, pricing, inventory, and contract data remain private.
- Never promise subsidies, savings, yield, or payback without dated inputs and assumptions.
