# Vibeclubs Design

Vibeclubs should feel like an alive room: social, warm, rhythmic, and easy to join.

## Interface Rules

- Prioritize people, events, drops, rooms, and participation.
- Use warm contrast and clear membership signals.
- Motion can pulse, queue, count down, and celebrate, but repeated flows must remain fast.
- Keep CTAs human: join, host, claim, invite, remix, show up.

## Typography

- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque, 600–800, for room names, event titles, drops, and social calls to action. Its role is expressive and rhythmic rather than institutional.
- **Body/UI:** Inter, 400–700, for membership flows, schedules, profiles, and participation copy.
- **Editorial:** Playfair Display is not a default Vibeclubs font. Use it only for a deliberately hosted editorial or cultural-program format, never as inherited portfolio styling.
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono only when a real code, ticket, timestamp, or aligned-data use case requires it.
- **Portfolio Boundary:** Vibeclubs shares the readable support layer, not the FrankX/Starlight display identity. It should feel social, kinetic, and welcoming at a glance.

## Avoid

- Sterile SaaS dashboards.
- Overly dark nightclub cliches.
- Confusing ritual language that hides the next action.
