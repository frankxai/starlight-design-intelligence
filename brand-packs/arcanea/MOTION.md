# Arcanea Motion

Arcanea motion is creative intelligence as ritual. It should feel authored, luminous, tactile, and transformational. The user is not watching decoration; they are invoking a system, opening a world, and shaping an artifact.

## Personality

- Mythic, not vague fantasy.
- Cinematic, not slow.
- Luminous, not overglowed.
- Tactile, not flat.
- Ceremonial, not cumbersome.

## Signature Behaviors

| Behavior | Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glyph trace | Revealing symbols, paths, relationships, canon links | Decorative scribbles with no semantic role |
| Portal reveal | Opening worlds, collections, lesson modules, story layers | Full-screen gimmicks that hide navigation |
| Artifact focus | Cards, tools, templates, characters, lore objects | Floating objects without hierarchy |
| Invocation sequence | First-run actions, generation moments, ritual onboarding | Repeating long sequences in everyday workflows |
| Constellation lore | World graphs, canon relationships, creative maps | Random node explosions |

## Tokens

```text
duration.micro: 120-180ms
duration.base: 240-360ms
duration.ritual: 500-900ms
duration.cinematic: 900-1600ms
stagger.glyph: 30-55ms
stagger.ceremony: 80-140ms
distance.touch: 3-6px
distance.reveal: 12-28px
```

Use emphasized ease-out for reveal, soft constrained springs for tactile artifacts, and longer cinematic timing only for first-run, launch, or story beats.

## Product Surfaces

- Arcanea Studio: expressive creative workspace, fast enough for repeated making.
- Academy/onboarding: ritualized progression with clear steps and visible progress.
- World repository: canon layers, lore cards, maps, and relationship graphs.
- Marketplace/gallery: rarity, provenance, collection, ownership, and preview motion.
- Launch pages: one immersive signature behavior with restrained interactive depth.

## Reduced Motion

Preserve the ceremony through final-state composition, hierarchy, copy, and light. Replace travel with direct reveals, static keyframes, shorter fades, and user-triggered playback.

## Brand Boundary

Do not use Arcanea mythic motion on FrankX or enterprise Starlight surfaces unless the target explicitly belongs to Arcanea.
