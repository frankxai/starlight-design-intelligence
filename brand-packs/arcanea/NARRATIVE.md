# An inheritance that asks for a choice

Apply [honor and responsibility](../../playbooks/honor-and-responsibility.md) within the existing [brand](BRAND.md), [copy](COPY.md) and [source authority](SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md). Arcanea's authored fiction may express the principle through a vow, inherited craft or a consequence. Its creator-tool interface remains clear and usable.

**Reader and job:** A creator or reader wants a meaningful choice inside an authored world, with consequences they can understand.

**Promise:** Let inherited power become responsibility through a choice and its cost.

**Behavior:** Give a character something received, a boundary on its use, a decision they can refuse, and a consequence that changes what someone else will inherit. Let repair cost something. Keep the creator's source and authorship visible outside the fictional frame.

**Tension:** Reverence for what came before can become obedience. A character earns agency by choosing what to preserve, change or end; lineage does not establish superiority or permission to harm others.

**Proof:** A scene with observable choices and consequences, reviewed against existing canon and its sources. New lore stays a proposal until the canon owner accepts it. Fiction does not supply claims about the author's ancestry or a real person's spiritual authority.

## Copy specimen: non-canonical craft exercise

> An apprentice inherits a tool and the harm left by its last use. Write the moment they choose whether to preserve its purpose, change its use, or refuse it. Make that choice cost something. End with what the next keeper receives.

This is a fiction-writing prompt, not a new event, character, artifact or rule in Arcanea canon. It invents no named lineage or sacred authority. The learner chooses the setting and submits a proposal through the owning canon workflow.

## Story and tool contracts

- Connect vows to action, refusal and consequence. Avoid a chosen-one status that excuses a character's choices.
- Separate existing canon, sourced interpretation and proposed additions in review records. Cite the source revision for existing facts.
- Show repair through changed behavior and consequences; a declaration of forgiveness is not evidence that harm was repaired.
- Keep mythic diction inside authored Arcanea work. Product controls name the actual action and state, with ordinary language for permissions and recovery.
- Do not publish private family stories or new lore through this public pack. Route unpublished material to the private creative backlog.

The name-swap distinction is dramatic causality: an inherited object, a refused or accepted responsibility, a costly choice and a changed future. The specimen teaches story craft without asserting canon.
