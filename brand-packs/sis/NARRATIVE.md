# Intelligence people can direct

Apply [honor and responsibility](../../playbooks/honor-and-responsibility.md) within the existing Starlight [brand modes](BRAND.md), [product behavior](PRODUCT.md) and [content system](CONTENT.md). Warmth, curiosity and possibility remain part of the brand. The operational specimen below belongs to Operator mode.

**Reader and job:** A person coordinating consequential work needs to direct capable intelligence and understand what remains their decision.

**Promise:** Make the next useful contribution possible, with enough evidence and control to take responsibility for it.

**Behavior:** Bound the mission, identify its owner and authority, expose source evidence and uncertainty, allow intervention, and return usable work with a resumable handover. Keep budgets and uncompleted dependencies visible where they affect a decision.

**Tension:** A wider horizon requires ambition; accepting a particular result requires evidence. Seamless automation can conceal the moment when a person needs to choose.

**Proof:** A real mission brief, inspected output, relevant trace or check, human decision and portable handover at the application revision under review. A proposed control or diagram is not proof that the product implements it.

## Copy specimen

> Set the mission. Inspect what comes back. Decide what moves forward.
>
> Before accepting this result, review its sources, checks and open questions. The handover should give the next owner enough context to continue.

Use this only beside an actual result and review controls. If the application lacks a control, record the dependency rather than rendering a simulated success state.

## Expression by mode

- **Horizon:** Connect inherited knowledge and long ambition to a frontier people can enter. Show a real contribution opportunity; label vision as vision.
- **Operator:** Put authority, evidence, intervention and recovery at the point of decision. A requested stop is not a completed stop; an accepted result is not necessarily deployed.
- **Academy:** Help learners practice judgment and retain their portfolio. Show observed work; do not certify mastery from activity counts.
- **Cultural:** Honor the artists, sources and communities behind the work. Wonder may be poetic; attribution and claims about real people remain factual and consent-based.

The name-swap distinction is accountable coordination: mission, authority, inspection, owner decision and handover. This does not replace the existing public proposition or turn every Starlight surface into an operations dashboard.
