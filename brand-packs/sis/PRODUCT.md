# Starlight / SIS Product

SIS product surfaces should help operators understand what agents are doing, where risk lives, and what decision is needed next.

## Core Surfaces

- Command center: global state, active work, alerts, owners, and next actions.
- Eval lab: test runs, score trends, regression evidence, and quality gates.
- Swarm cockpit: agents, queues, handoffs, escalation, and human-gated decisions.
- Knowledge graph: entities, claims, references, provenance, and relationship depth.

## Product Rules

- Show status and ownership everywhere.
- Make failures inspectable.
- Keep primary actions clear and reversible.
- Prefer dense, stable, scannable UI over cinematic marketing composition.
- Include audit trails and proof artifacts near decisions.
