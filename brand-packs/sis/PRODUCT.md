# Starlight Intelligence Product

Starlight products help people understand complex systems, work with capable agents, build meaningful artifacts, and see how their contribution fits into a larger future.

## Product promise

The user should leave each meaningful session with clearer understanding, stronger capability, or completed work that matters to them.

## Core experiences

### Intelligence workbench

The workbench is where a person collaborates with named agents, sources, tools, and durable projects. It preserves decisions, artifacts, progress, and provenance across sessions.

### Command center

The command center shows active work, owners, cost, quality, risk, and the decisions that need human attention. It provides control without requiring the user to understand every implementation detail.

### Learning atlas

The atlas connects skills, mentors, projects, evidence of mastery, and a portfolio the learner owns. Progress becomes credible through completed work and growing independence.

### Contribution graph

The graph connects people, agents, ventures, research, cultural works, and outcomes. It helps the user see where their work came from, what it influenced, and which opportunities are open next.

### Frontier map

The frontier map presents important problems, active research, venture opportunities, and ways to contribute. It gives the civilizational mission an accessible interface.

## Product behavior

- Explain what the system is doing in language appropriate to the user's role.
- Keep ownership, authority, sources, cost, and uncertainty available at the point of decision.
- Let users inspect and correct memory, identity, and contribution records.
- Design graceful recovery into every consequential workflow.
- Make the next meaningful action easy to recognize.
- Preserve user-owned artifacts in portable forms wherever practical.
- Teach through use. Repeated interaction should increase the user's judgment.

## Agent relationships

Agents have stable identities, clear roles, and bounded authority. They may feel warm, distinctive, and alive. Their usefulness comes from the quality of the relationship and the work they help complete.

A teacher agent should gradually require less scaffolding. A research agent should make its reasoning and sources easier to inspect. A cultural entity should deepen the world rather than generate endless interchangeable content.

## Failure and recovery

Failures are stated plainly. The product explains what was preserved, what remains uncertain, and what the user can do next. High-consequence actions use confirmation, receipts, and rollback paths. Support boundaries remain visible.

## Product modes

Operator products prioritize control and legibility. Academy products prioritize practice and portfolio growth. Horizon experiences prioritize orientation, possibility, and contribution. Cultural products prioritize continuity, expression, and belonging.

The modes may connect inside one ecosystem. Each screen still chooses one primary job.

## Release gate

A release should demonstrate useful activation, technical reliability, accessible interaction, coherent brand behavior, inspectable agent authority, and a clear path for the user to become more capable. A beautiful interface cannot compensate for a broken value loop.
