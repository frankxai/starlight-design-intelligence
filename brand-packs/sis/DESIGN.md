# Starlight / SIS Design

> **Foundation reset — 2026-08-17:** This pack is a legacy hypothesis, not an approved identity. Do not treat command centers, dark-neutral bases, celestial language, status chips, or technical typography as brand defaults. The active source is `brand-image-system/foundation-reset/2026-08-17/`; begin with the Civic Intelligence territory and a founder-reviewed wordmark/type specimen. Sentence case is mandatory.

Starlight Intelligence Systems should feel like a high-trust command layer for agentic work: precise, calm, legible, evidence-rich, and quietly powerful.

## Visual Principles

- Show structure before spectacle.
- Make system state visible: running, blocked, verifying, escalating, complete.
- Use density responsibly: compact information, clear grouping, stable navigation.
- Let evidence feel tactile: traces, receipts, logs, proofs, scores, and references should be inspectable.
- Use celestial language only as information architecture: constellation, signal, trace, orbit, horizon, field.

## Interface Defaults

- Layout: dense but breathable dashboards, command-center panels, timelines, and graphs.
- Color: dark-neutral or high-contrast operational bases with restrained cyan/teal/signal accents.
- Typography: use the shared four-role system—Poppins display, Inter interface/body, Playfair editorial, and JetBrains Mono evidence—so the system feels authored without sacrificing operational clarity.
- Components: use tabs, tables, timelines, inspector panes, status chips, or command bars only when a real product task requires them; they are not marketing or identity decoration.
- Imagery: real product states, evidence screenshots, system diagrams, not abstract glow.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic space backgrounds.
- Excessive gradients, particles, or glowing orbs.
- Marketing cards that hide operational density.
- Motion that delays operators or obscures logs.
- Mythic Arcanea language on enterprise/SIS surfaces.

## Typography Standard

The shared editorial serif is **Playfair Display**, loaded locally through the framework font pipeline. It is the preferred serif across Starlight marketing, research, accord, academy, and long-form surfaces because it gives the system a clear human voice without becoming ornamental or mythic.

- **Display:** Poppins, 600–800; use for primary product headings, navigation anchors, and high-signal calls to action.
- **Interface/body:** Inter, 300–700; use for navigation, controls, forms, dense descriptions, and operational labels.
- **Editorial:** Playfair Display, 400–600; use for mission, thesis, explanatory section headings, pull quotes, and slow-reading passages.
- **Evidence/readouts:** JetBrains Mono, 400–600; use for timestamps, receipts, statuses, identifiers, and technical metadata.
- **Ratio:** serif should carry the meaning-bearing 20–35% of a surface, not every label or paragraph.
- **Exceptions:** Arcanea may use Cormorant Garamond for its mythic world-building register; product controls remain sans/mono. FrankX may use the same Playfair role with its own sans pairing.

When a page is primarily editorial, the serif may carry the main heading and body reading measure. When a page is primarily operational, use it for the page thesis and section headings only so evidence remains fast to scan.

## Star and Wordmark Device

Starlight may use a custom star-derived **A** in the `Starlight` wordmark as a signature device. This is a controlled identity asset, not a text substitution.

- Use the star-A only in an approved vector wordmark, hero lockup, opening title, favicon family, or rare campaign signature.
- Keep the accessible name, alt text, metadata, URLs, and running copy spelled `Starlight`; never replace the letter with `★`, `✦`, `✧`, or another Unicode glyph in ordinary text.
- Construct the star-A as deliberate editable vector geometry and test it at 16px, 32px, navigation size, social avatar, print, and monochrome before approval.
- Allow at most one dominant star gesture per composition. Supporting stars must communicate orientation, provenance, progression, or a real celestial reference—not generic sparkle filler.
- Do not scatter star fields behind dashboards, repeat sparkles around CTAs, or turn every `A` into the device.
- The star-A remains founder-gated identity work. A CSS approximation, typed symbol, generated raster, or unreviewed inline SVG is not the production mark.
