## Application rules

- Preserve the four-point silhouette, luminous center in the color variant and
  companion dot. Use the wordmark beside the mark in navigation.
- Use the color mark at 24px or larger; use monochrome at 16px. Clear space is
  one quarter of the mark width. The supplied master has a 48 by 48 viewBox.
- Use the lab's Instrument Sans, Instrument Serif italic and IBM Plex Mono in
  their declared roles. Exact font files, licenses, embedding and computed
  browser fonts still require owning-product proof.
- The lab palette is scoped to `.ai`. Preserve Academy and protocol reading
  treatments. Companion cyan and gold must not become body text on light paper.
- Optional cursor motion keeps the native cursor, four reusable stars and idle
  fade. Suppress it for reduced motion, forced colors, touch, forms, dragging,
  selections and dialogs. These are owning requirements, not verified behavior.
- Preserve current vectors and historical studies. New exports require their own
  truthful sidecars and ledger entries. The historical sidecar above uses a
  legacy shape and has not been validated against the current VIS schema.

## Observation and adoption limits

On 4 October 2026 the lab `/brand`, identity manifest and three SVG downloads
returned HTTP 200. Manifest and SVG response bytes matched the pinned Git blobs.
This establishes availability and source agreement only. Rendered specimens,
small-size/background/mask proof, keyboard/mobile interactions, rights,
computed fonts, deployment revision and all three sites' synchronization remain
unverified in this slice. No new visual was generated or exported.

The serious alternative is to replace all Starlight palettes and marks with
one global lab default. The owning contracts preserve site palettes and the
protocol variant, so the pack uses scoped site references and compatible
contract identifiers. Existing operator color keys remain for compatibility
and carry no current-site adoption assertion.

The pack schema and portfolio validator verify structure, repository membership
and supported mode identifiers. They do not resolve site tokens, inspect visual
quality, install fonts or apply product code. A downstream proposal must retain
its local contracts, pin a reviewed kernel revision and pass the actual product
checks. Independent review and the named human promotion gate remain required.

Rollback is the prior SIS pack and DESIGN Git blobs; owning product sources,
historical variants and downstream pins are outside this change.
