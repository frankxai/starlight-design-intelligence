# Starlight Intelligence Brand Constitution

Starlight Intelligence helps people and organizations use advanced intelligence to build a more capable, creative, and far-reaching civilization.

Starlight is a place for curious people and capable agents to explore, discover, and build together. Its intelligence should feel powerful, its tools beautiful, and its invitation open. Public work makes possibility vivid. Product work gives people a clear way to act on it.

## Promise

Starlight brings agents, tools, and context together so people can turn a question into a discovery and an idea into something real.

## Audience

Starlight serves builders, researchers, creators, operators, educators, founders, institutions, and curious people who want to do consequential work with modern intelligence.

The brand does not chase audiences looking only for shortcuts, synthetic status, or passive spectacle. Entertainment and wonder are welcome when they open a real emotional or creative horizon.

## Narrative

Humanity is still early. We have inherited extraordinary tools and equally extraordinary problems. Intelligence can help us discover new knowledge, regenerate the planet, create better institutions, deepen community, and eventually travel far beyond Earth.

Starlight exists to help more people see themselves inside that story. It turns advanced technology into practical capability, and it turns long-horizon ambition into work people can begin now.

## Surface modes

### Horizon

Use Horizon mode for public stories, open experiments, research frontiers, cultural work, and major launches. The voice is expansive, vivid, warm, and future-facing. Every vision should still reveal a path people can enter.

### Operator

Use Operator mode for SIS, governance, enterprise architecture, agent operations, evaluation, and command surfaces. The voice is precise, direct, warm, and human. Explain capability through visible work, clear decisions, and outcomes.

### Academy

Use Academy mode for learning, skill progression, mentors, and student portfolios. The voice is intelligent, generous, and developmental. Learners should feel challenged, respected, and increasingly independent.

### Cultural

Use Cultural mode for music, characters, stories, gatherings, and Starlight rituals. The voice may become poetic and emotionally charged. It remains coherent with the canon and never borrows authority from fictional experience.

## Voice

Read `COPY.md` and `VOICE.json` for the shared voice contract. Curiosity, confidence, warmth, and occasional wit apply to every mode. A mode changes emphasis, not personality.


Write like a brilliant human who has spent time with the problem. Use complete sentences, natural rhythm, and concrete consequences. Let luminous language appear where the thought earns it.

Starlight can speak about superintelligence, the soul, civilization, and the stars. Those words should connect to technology, research, art, community, or a practice people can actually join.

Avoid synthetic manifesto cadence, generic enterprise language, repeated contrast formulas, and piles of abstract nouns. The brand should never sound like a governance consultancy wearing a space-themed skin.

## Core messages

1. Intelligence should expand what people can understand and create.
2. Every person can contribute to a future larger than their current role.
3. Human and machine intelligence become more valuable through wise coordination.
4. Culture gives technological progress direction, courage, and meaning.
5. The path to the stars begins with better work, stronger communities, and responsible choices on Earth.

## Proof style

Horizon claims are framed as vision. Product claims show the mechanism. Scientific and social claims preserve their sources and limits. Customer and community stories retain consent and context.

## Guardrails

Starlight loses its identity when it becomes cold compliance language, generic cosmic decoration, artificial spiritual certainty, or another collection of AI features. It also loses credibility when it claims civilizational impact without showing any meaningful work beneath the claim.

The desired feeling is a beautiful workshop of capable minds: a place to explore a frontier, find brilliant company, and make something worth sharing.
