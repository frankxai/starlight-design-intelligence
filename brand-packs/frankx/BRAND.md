# FrankX Brand

FrankX is the practical founder-facing edge of the ecosystem: strategic, direct, commercially literate, and useful.

## Promise

Help builders turn AI capability into owned systems, products, revenue loops, and leverage.

## Voice

- Clear, sharp, founder-to-founder.
- Practical before poetic.
- Strategic but not corporate.
- Confident claims backed by mechanism, examples, or operational detail.

## Avoid

- Mythic Arcanea language.
- Enterprise fog.
- Empty hustle copy.
- Generic "unlock your potential" phrasing.
