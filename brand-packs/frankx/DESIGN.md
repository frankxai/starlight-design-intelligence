# FrankX Brand Design System

> Source of truth for the FrankX visual identity. Built for high restraint, technical rigor, and humble excellence.
> Integrates the Tech spectrum (emerald/cyan) and Soul spectrum (amber/gold) over a deep obsidian foundation.

---

## 1 · Core Thesis & Persona
FrankX is a personal authority brand and developer/creator funnel.
- **Tone:** Technical, direct, warm, playful, systems-oriented.
- **Visual Personality:** High-restraint editorial layouts, crisp typography, clean data lines, and glowing accent systems.

---

## 2 · The Two Spectra (Colors)

### A. Tech Spectrum (Emerald + Cyan)
Used for technical content, architecture diagrams, code interfaces, and primary calls to action.
- **Void (Background):** `#0a0a0b` (Deep obsidian black, never pure black)
- **Space (Elevated Surface):** `#111113`
- **Elevated:** `#1a1a1f`
- **Tech Primary:** `#10b981` (Emerald-500: Active CTAs and technical accents)
- **Tech Secondary:** `#06b6d4` (Cyan-500: Highlights and data lines)
- **Tech Glow:** `#0f1f1a` (Emerald at 15% opacity over void)

### B. Soul Spectrum (Amber + Gold)
Used for contemplative journals, essays, music, and the Soulbook register.
- **Soul Primary:** `#f59e0b` (Amber-500: Warmth, music, editorial focus)
- **Soul Secondary:** `#fbbf24` (Amber-400: Warm highlights)
- **Soul Glow:** `#241b0f` (Amber at 15% opacity over void)
- **Contemplative Ink (Editorial):** `#e8ddd0` (Warm cream text on obsidian void)

---

## 3 · Typography
- **Display Headlines:** Poppins (Regular 700–800) for displays ≥18px. Generous weight contrast.
- **Body Text:** Inter (Regular 400, line-height 1.6–1.7). High legibility.
- **Labels & Eyebrows:** Inter (Medium 500), tracking +0.25em, uppercase.
- **Data & Mono:** JetBrains Mono for system outputs, metrics, and code nodes.

---

## 4 · Visual Design Guidelines
- **Restraint:** Favor negative space. Never clutter. Use thin lines (`#1e1e1e` border, `#2f2f2f` strong border) for separation.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric, clean margins, strong grid alignment.
- **Forbidden:** Neon gradients, pure white on pure black, drop shadows on flat surfaces, and bloated UI frames.
