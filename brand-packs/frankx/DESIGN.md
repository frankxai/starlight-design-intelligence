# FrankX Brand Design System

> Source of truth for FrankX surfaces. FrankX does not have one visual costume.
> Choose the surface mode before choosing color, type, imagery, or motion.

---

## 1 · Experience Thesis

FrankX makes ambitious systems legible and usable. A surface should feel authored by
a perceptive builder—not generated by an AI landing-page template.

- Start with a real person, artifact, observation, or working scene.
- Put human language in front. Keep agent, skill, graph, and infrastructure taxonomy backstage until it explains proof.
- Prefer one memorable composition to a wall of interchangeable cards.
- Use restraint to create authority; never use abstraction to conceal a weak proposition.

Read `SURFACE_MODES.md` before implementation.

---

## 2 · Color

The obsidian spectrum belongs to product and technical surfaces. It is not the
default for a personal note, partner page, editorial profile, or relationship
surface. Those surfaces begin with the source material and may use paper, daylight,
warm neutral, or photographic palettes.

```yaml
colors:
  # Foundation
  void: "#0a0a0b"              # obsidian page background
  space: "#111113"             # primary elevated surfaces
  elevated: "#1a1a1f"          # secondary elevated cards
  border: "#1e1e1e"            # white at 8% opacity over void
  border-strong: "#2f2f2f"     # white at 15% opacity over void
  
  # Tech Spectrum (emerald + cyan) — AI & Technical Rigor
  tech-primary: "#10b981"      # emerald-500: Primary CTAs
  tech-secondary: "#06b6d4"    # cyan-500: highlights, data lines
  tech-glow: "#0f1f1a"         # emerald at 15% over void
  
  # Soul Spectrum (amber + gold) — Soulbook, Warmth, Music
  soul-primary: "#f59e0b"      # amber-500: editorial highlights
  soul-secondary: "#fbbf24"    # amber-400: warm highlights
  soul-glow: "#241b0f"         # amber at 15% over void
```

## 3 · Typography

Typography is selected, not inherited by reflex.

- **Product/technical default:** Inter for interface text and JetBrains Mono for data. Poppins may remain as a compatibility display face on existing product surfaces.
- **Editorial/relationship:** select a licensed text/display pairing from actual specimens. Poppins is a fallback, not a flagship decision.
- Use no more than two expressive families plus one monospaced utility face.
- Test names, numerals, short headings, long paragraphs, italics, and mobile wraps before approval.
- Record source, license, weights, fallbacks, and loading behavior in release evidence.
- Do not use faux italics or weights the files do not contain.

---

## 4 · Composition

- Give the first viewport one clear subject and one reason to continue.
- Let evidence interrupt prose: a photograph, annotated artifact, working trace, quotation, or specific result.
- Avoid repeated equal-weight cards, floating glass panels, ornamental grids, and dashboard chrome on editorial surfaces.
- An asymmetric editorial composition is preferable when it creates hierarchy without reducing legibility.
- Every route needs a designed mobile composition, not a compressed desktop layout.

---

## 5 · Image and Asset Production

- Use owned, authorized, or clearly licensed assets.
- For a named person, never synthesize likeness, biography, quotation, relationship, or endorsement as evidence.
- Generated media must pass `evals/generated-asset-quality-gate.md` after export inspection.
- Record source, rights, transformation, and publication status in repository-relative release evidence.
- Visual-generation tools are optional producers, never sources of truth.

## 6 · Release Bar

A flagship surface cannot ship until:

- the current desktop and mobile experience is captured;
- exactly three materially different directions are compared;
- the selected direction has editorial, typography, motion, responsive, accessibility, and performance evidence;
- a verifier who did not make the work signs the visual result;
- the production URL, commit, checks, and rollback target are recorded.
