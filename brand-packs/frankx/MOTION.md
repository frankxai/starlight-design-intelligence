# FrankX Motion Language

Motion earns its place by improving orientation, causality, focus, explanation,
confirmation, or memory. “Premium feel” is not a motion job.

## Contract

Every motion sequence must name:

- the job it performs;
- the visual anchor that remains stable;
- the controlled property and duration or scroll range;
- who controls or interrupts it;
- the reduced-motion equivalent;
- the performance budget and verification method.

## Surface Behavior

- **Editorial / relationship:** one authored evidence sequence at most. Favor direct
  manipulation, a documentary transition, or the assembly of a real artifact.
  Never animate prose line by line.
- **Product / technical:** animate state, delta, progress, and causal connection.
  Keep input response short and interruptible.
- **Soul / cultural:** longer rhythm is allowed when it serves attention or emotion,
  but the static composition must remain complete.

## Runtime Defaults

- Prefer transform and opacity.
- Avoid persistent ambient motion behind reading text.
- Preserve focus, selection, scrolling, and input responsiveness.
- Treat `prefers-reduced-motion` as a designed state, not a blanket removal that
  leaves broken composition.
- Ship one signature sequence before adding secondary polish.

## Cut Rule

If a reviewer cannot state what the motion makes easier to understand or remember,
cut it.
