# FrankX Surface Modes

Choose one primary mode before designing. A page may borrow a secondary register,
but it may not collapse all FrankX work into the same dark technical costume.

## Editorial / Relationship

Use for partner pages, personal notes, profiles, essays, invitations, and MVU pages.

- Begin with a person, remembered moment, or authorized artifact.
- Favor warm neutral, paper, daylight, documentary, or source-led palettes.
- Typography carries most of the identity; select it from licensed specimens.
- Use one editorial composition and one evidence scene.
- Navigation and global ecosystem chrome should recede.
- Motion may reveal causality or a working artifact; prose stays still.
- The page ends with one bounded human next move.

## Product / Technical

Use for tools, dashboards, documentation, repositories, and system explanations.

- Obsidian, emerald, cyan, grids, and monospace are available.
- Put the working interface or trace in the first viewport.
- Use panels only where the information architecture requires panels.
- Motion explains state, causality, progress, or spatial relationship.
- Product terminology is welcome after the task and proof are clear.

## Soul / Cultural

Use for music, reflection, canon, embodied work, and long-form cultural pieces.

- Warm dark or tactile palettes are available.
- Rhythm, image, quotation, and silence lead.
- Serif or humanist typography must be licensed and specimen-tested.
- Never import Arcanea mythology unless the route explicitly belongs to Arcanea.

## Failure Modes

- Applying obsidian plus cyan glow to every route.
- A centered slogan followed by an identical card grid.
- A personal invitation written like product documentation.
- A technical page with no working technical evidence.
- Decorative motion competing with the subject.
