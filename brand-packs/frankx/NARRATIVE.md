# Work someone else can learn from

Apply [honor and responsibility](../../playbooks/honor-and-responsibility.md) within the existing FrankX [brand](BRAND.md), [editorial language](COPY.md) and [surface modes](SURFACE_MODES.md). Keep FrankX practical and founder-facing.

**Reader and job:** A builder wants to understand a useful result well enough to make the next one independently.

**Promise:** Show the work and the judgment that made it useful.

**Behavior:** Explain what was received, what changed and what the reader can carry forward. Link an inspectable example, name the source revision, show a rejected alternative and offer a transfer exercise. Credit people and sources without inventing a personal anecdote.

**Tension:** Teaching earns authority while giving the reader less reason to depend on the teacher. A polished outcome can hide the decisions that made it work.

**Proof:** A real artifact, its relevant checks, one decision the reader can inspect and an exercise that changes the problem rather than copying the answer. A repository link proves location; it does not by itself prove a working result or a learner outcome.

## Copy specimen

> A useful build should leave you with more than a finished file. It should show the source, the decision that changed the result, and the checks that caught a mistake. Inspect the example, then change one assumption and make your own version. Keep the method you can explain.

This is teaching copy for a build with that evidence. Attach the actual example and source credit before publication; never use it to imply an unseen build passed.

## Content and product contracts

- Founder essays turn gratitude into concrete commitments about attribution, quality, honest limits, correction and handover. They speak from owner-supplied intent, not an invented family history.
- A tutorial names prerequisites, uses an example that can be inspected or run, states what its checks establish, and gives the learner a meaningful variation.
- A build review distinguishes generated output, human changes, observed behavior and unresolved work. The artifact stays usable outside the story about it.
- Corrections preserve the original claim's context and point readers to the corrected result. Praise, testimonials and learning outcomes need their own authorized evidence.

The specimen passes the name-swap test through its teaching mechanism: an inspected build, visible judgment and transfer practice. It is not Starlight mission copy, GenCreator edition copy, Arcanea canon or an earnings offer.
