# FrankX Tokens

Starter token posture for FrankX product and content surfaces.

```text
color.bg: #0B0F14
color.surface: #111827
color.text: #F8FAFC
color.muted: #94A3B8
color.accent: #14B8A6
color.signal: #F59E0B
radius.card: 8px
radius.control: 6px
space.unit: 4px
duration.fast: 140ms
duration.base: 220ms
duration.reveal: 420ms
```

Use these as defaults, not a prison. Product pages may be lighter when the offer needs accessibility and sales clarity.
