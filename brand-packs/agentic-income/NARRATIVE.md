# Useful work with full economics

Apply [honor and responsibility](../../playbooks/honor-and-responsibility.md) within the existing [brand](BRAND.md) and [design](DESIGN.md). Preserve Earn, Automate, Compound and the boundary against earnings promises.

**Reader and job:** An independent operator needs to decide whether an agent service delivers useful work at a cost they can sustain.

**Promise:** Make the delivered work and the economics inspectable before asking someone to rely on the offer.

**Behavior:** Define the buyer's acceptance criteria, test the service, retain failed attempts and rework, and record model spend, tools, fees and human labor. Identify who owns the result and what needs intervention.

**Tension:** A successful demonstration can be technically real and economically misleading if discarded attempts or unpaid review labor disappear from the account.

**Proof:** Dated artifacts and an all-attempt ledger with quality acceptance, rejected attempts, costs, refunds and labor assumptions. Revenue, cash collected, margin and profit are different measures. Synthetic examples remain labelled and cannot establish customer demand.

## Copy specimen

> Inspect the delivery before you copy the business.
>
> Open the accepted artifact and the attempt ledger. Include the runs that failed, the time spent correcting them, and the costs that remained after the work was accepted. Then decide whether this service fits your own buyer and limits.

Use this beside a real case or clearly labelled synthetic exercise. No inspected case means no outcome claim. Paid access remains unavailable until the existing checkout and delivery gates pass.

## Offer and case contracts

- Name the customer's job, output, acceptance test, resource ceiling and delivery limit before execution.
- Record every attempt under the same case ID. Retry, review, rework and refund costs remain in the account.
- Publish the date, denominator, currency and treatment of labor next to an economic claim. Distinguish measured figures from estimates and assumptions.
- Describe corrections and their effect on cost and quality. Do not erase a failed attempt to improve the apparent margin.
- Preserve the skill, schema, scaffold, validator and handover so another operator can inspect the method. Disclose affiliate relationships where applicable.

The name-swap distinction is full delivery economics: acceptance, all attempts, labor and limits. It offers a testable service method, not a lifestyle or guaranteed income.
