# AnimeLegends Brand

AnimeLegends is an original character world with Studio, Atlas, and Forge as three distinct public doors.

## Promise

The world leads; scholarship and inspectable production tools deepen it without presenting drafts as releases.

## Boundaries

- AKASHI, KAGE, and MIRA remain original canon.
- Mascot canon is outside the MIT Forge license.
- Every generated or commissioned asset needs provenance and an inspected release state.
- Do not imitate an existing anime franchise, mark, character, or composition.
