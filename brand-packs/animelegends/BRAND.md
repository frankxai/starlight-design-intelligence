# AnimeLegends Brand

AnimeLegends is an original character world with Studio, Atlas, and Forge as three distinct public doors.

## Promise

The world leads; scholarship and inspectable production tools deepen it without presenting drafts as releases.

## Cultural ambition

Build an original world people care enough about to follow, discuss, and remember. Recognition begins with a character's desire, a difficult choice, and a consequence. Production technology should deepen the work rather than become its premise.

The audience enters through story and character. Creators and researchers can then enter through how that work is made and understood. Preserve that order.

Proposed editorial line: **Every legend begins with a choice.** The accompanying scene must reveal an actual choice from approved original canon. A dramatic sentence cannot substitute for a story.

## Three doors, three promises

- **Studio:** encounter the original work. Lead with a released scene, character, or edition whose status is explicit.
- **Atlas:** understand the world and its references. Separate original canon, interpretation, and sourced scholarship.
- **Forge:** inspect and use production tools. Show inputs, outputs, license scope, and a reproducible example.

The same character should remain recognizable across a scene, a still, a profile, and a production study. Consistency includes motive and behavior as well as appearance.

## Voice and wording

Write with dramatic specificity in stories and editorial clarity around them. Let dialogue carry character. Keep navigation simple: “Watch the scene,” “Meet the characters,” “Explore the Atlas,” or “Inspect the Forge,” when those destinations are available.

Avoid announcing that the work is epic, legendary, or franchise-defining. Let the stakes of a specific scene establish scale. Never write a trailer as though an unreleased series already exists.

## Boundaries

- AKASHI, KAGE, and MIRA remain original canon.
- Mascot canon is outside the MIT Forge license.
- Every generated or commissioned asset needs provenance and an inspected release state.
- Do not imitate an existing anime franchise, mark, character, or composition.

## Proof and product behavior

Maintain canon continuity, asset lineage, accessible captions, clear release labels, and persistent links to editions. Measure return to stories and characters, comprehension of their stakes, and engagement with released work. Asset volume alone is not a cultural outcome.
