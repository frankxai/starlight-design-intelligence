# AI CoE / AI Architect Academy Brand

AI CoE is the canonical pack for AI Architect Academy and future Center of Excellence surfaces.

## Promise

Learn AI architecture by building inspectable systems with an AI instructor, governed exercises, and pass/fail evidence.

## Ambition

Develop architects whose judgment survives contact with a real system. The learner should become able to choose an approach, explain its limits, test it under pressure, and improve it without depending on the instructor.

Proposed line: **Learn to make the architecture decisions.** Support it with a lab that presents a consequential choice, not a catalog of subjects.

## Signature learning

The learning unit is a design decision with executable evidence: a constraint, alternatives, an implementation, a failure case, and a review. Progress should reveal stronger judgment and greater independence. Completion badges may summarize evidence; they cannot replace it.

Academy speaks to the individual learning the craft. CoE speaks to an organization developing a repeatable capability: ownership, shared practices, review, and operating outcomes. Keep the relationship clear without making every lesson sound like procurement.

## Voice and product behavior

Be exact, generous, and demanding. “Defend your design” belongs beside an explicit rubric. “Run the failure case” belongs beside a runnable fixture. Feedback should identify the decision to revisit and the evidence needed.

An exercise must start from a clean environment, state its prerequisites and cost, and distinguish a hint from a completed solution. Assess transfer with a changed scenario, not a repeated answer. Never imply accreditation, job placement, or institutional endorsement without current proof.

## Boundaries

- Academy is coding-agent-native education; CoE is its enterprise operating expression.
- Lead with labs, source code, reviews, and decision records.
- Never imply affiliation with Oracle or replace proof with certification theater.
