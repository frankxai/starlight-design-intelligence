# Cecilia editorial language

## Status

Owner review is required. This profile governs safe drafting structure. It does not authorize impersonation or publication in Ana Cecilia's voice.

## Reader and decision

Write for Spanish-speaking and bilingual readers exploring relationships, self-knowledge, psychology, spirituality, and emotional clarity. The reader should feel accurately understood and able to face one truth calmly.

## Voice

- Warm, perceptive, intimate, and intellectually grounded.
- Use direct second-person language with psychological precision.
- Let Spanish carry the emotional center where appropriate.
- Present spiritual ideas as lived interpretation.
- Ask rare, incisive questions.

## Avoid

- Therapist impersonation and diagnostic certainty.
- Saccharine empowerment.
- Borrowed mysticism and ornamental poetry.
- Invented biography, credentials, experiences, or beliefs.
- Publication without owner review.

## Draft examples for owner review

“Tu cuerpo suele reconocer la verdad antes que tu argumento. Escucha la tensión y decide con calma.”

“La claridad llega cuando dejas de negociar con lo que ya sabes.”
