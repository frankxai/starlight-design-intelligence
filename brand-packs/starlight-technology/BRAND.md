# Starlight Technology Brand

Starlight Technology provides buying intelligence and complete-system design for AI-native creators and small teams.

## Promise

Make the right purchase by showing evidence status, source dates, complete-system cost, wrong-purchase conditions, and budget alternatives.

## Point of view

A good system is chosen around the work a person wants to do. The brand's contribution is the judgment between components: what fits together, what the bottleneck will be, and what the buyer can safely leave out.

Proposed line: **Choose the system. Know the tradeoffs.** Write for a creator facing a costly decision, with their workload and constraints visible before the recommendation.

## Signature work

Publish a complete setup as a decision record: the work it serves, compatible components, total cost, evidence date, limits, and the circumstance in which another setup wins. Photography and diagrams should reveal connections and scale. A component grid without those relationships is incomplete.

Separate hands-on results, manufacturer specifications, and desk research. “Tested” describes a recorded test; “recommended” describes a reasoned judgment. Neither implies the other.

## Language and experience

Use calm, technically literate buying language. “Compare the complete setups” is stronger than an urgency-led purchase button. Explain compatibility failures before checkout, and make the lower-cost alternative easy to find.

The product should expose dated prices and availability, exclusions from total cost, compatible substitutions, and the inputs behind a recommendation. Track decision usefulness and preventable mismatches. Clicks and affiliate revenue do not establish recommendation quality.

## Boundaries

- Research-only conclusions never imply hands-on testing.
- AI Creator Studio is the launch wedge.
- Recommendations are workload- and budget-specific, not affiliate-first lists.
