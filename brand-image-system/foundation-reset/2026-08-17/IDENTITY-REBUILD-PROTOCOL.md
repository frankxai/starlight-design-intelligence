# Identity rebuild protocol

## 0. Quarantine and inventory

- Preserve rejected work as experiment evidence; do not publish, repurpose, or use it as a style reference.
- Inventory every current logo, font file, token, color, recurring layout, icon set, and generated asset with its source and actual use.
- Label each item `keep for test`, `reassess`, `retire`, or `unknown`. “Canonical” is a provenance label only.
- Current default: FrankX, GenCreator, and Arcanea marks are `reassess`; SIS has no verified approved mark.

## 1. Strategic card

Before visuals, write one page per brand:

- audience and moment of need;
- tension the brand resolves;
- promise and proof;
- personality in behavior, not adjectives;
- category conventions to reject;
- relationship to the other brands;
- one sentence that must remain true in five years.

No territory proceeds if these answers can be swapped between brands.

## 2. Territory proofs

Produce two territories per brand. Each proof is black and white and contains:

- wordmark treatment using ordinary title case;
- one real product title;
- a two-line brand statement;
- a paragraph of real copy;
- one diagram or image-placement decision;
- desktop and mobile crops;
- a short explanation of the idea and its risks.

Color, glow, 3D, and generated imagery are withheld. The founder chooses, rejects, or combines directions and states why.

## 3. Typography trials

- Use official trial fonts or currently licensed files; do not purchase during exploration.
- Test three families or pairings against the same real specimen.
- Compare punctuation, lowercase voice, italics, numerals, long reading, UI labels, and small sizes.
- Reject a font that only looks good in a giant headline.
- Record license, source, available weights, web cost, language coverage, and fallback plan before adoption.

## 4. Wordmark and symbol

- Begin with the name. A symbol is optional and must solve a real recognition or application problem.
- Develop in vector, black and white, without effects.
- Test 16px, 32px, favicon, nav, avatar, product cover, print, embroidery, and one-color reproduction.
- Run a category-collision audit against obvious AI, creator, fantasy, consultancy, and software marks.
- Image models may create exploratory sketches only. Accepted geometry is redrawn and optically corrected by hand or deterministic vector tooling.
- Color and motion arrive only after the static one-color system works.

## 5. Application quartet

Before a complete brand guide, prove the identity on four demanding surfaces:

1. Website hero at desktop and mobile.
2. One real product cover with title, form, and audience.
3. One social composition at 4:5 plus its 1:1 crop.
4. One product or document surface: app screen, report cover and spread, deck, or onboarding page.

Each brand uses a different layout grammar. A color-swapped template is an automatic restart.

## 6. Image-world benchmark

Only after a territory and quartet are approved:

- Write one art-direction brief and one negative brief.
- Route the same brief through at most two suitable premium image lanes per round.
- Judge outputs blind before revealing the model.
- Use generation for subjects, scenes, illustration, and world material; use exact tools for type, logo, charts, UI, and claims.
- Track model, version when exposed, prompt, references, seed or job id when exposed, source files, edits, crop, time, and final decision.
- Stop after two failed rounds and revisit the brief rather than adding prompt adjectives.

The current benchmark candidates include Codex image generation, Google/Nano Banana Pro, Grok Imagine, Recraft for appropriate vector or brand exploration, Higgsfield for controlled people/product media, and deterministic Figma/code/Remotion lanes. Availability and model identity must be verified at runtime; no model is crowned globally.

## 7. Human-first review

The review sequence is:

1. Maker documents intent and strongest concern.
2. Hygiene check verifies dimensions, accessibility, provenance, and artifact integrity.
3. Independent critic evaluates strategy, typography, composition, distinctiveness, and category collision.
4. Founder returns `keep`, `combine`, `iterate`, or `reject`, with a short reason.
5. The ledger records what survives and the next hypothesis.

A numeric score may summarize a review, but cannot approve work. `reject` always sets the experiment decision to `restart`.

## 8. Scale gate

Do not produce a full product family until:

- one territory is founder-approved;
- type licensing is known;
- logo or wordmark tests pass;
- the application quartet passes without a shared template;
- the logo-off recognition test passes;
- one image lane has produced an approved, inspected result when imagery is needed;
- the brand has an approved light and dark/reversal behavior;
- experiment receipts are complete.

After that gate, create the product portfolio as a coherent family inside each brand, not as one family across every brand.
