DESIGN REVIEW — Cross-brand visual generation system that turns brand packs and product manifests into marketing assets

| Failure mode              | Exposure | The specific gap |
|---------------------------|----------|------------------|
| Orchestration & failure   | RED      | The same production loop framed, rendered, inspected, and scored its own work. There was no founder preference gate before a 47-product fanout and no failure path for “technically correct but aesthetically wrong.” |
| Context & memory          | RED      | Brand behavior is fragmented across packs, operating-unit prose, render scripts, trackers, and estate instructions. Those sources conflict, GenCreator lacks a central pack, and repeated dark-command language overwhelms the stated distinctions. |
| Integration               | YELLOW   | Media jobs and ledgers exist, but identity assets, model receipts, deterministic overlays, and human review do not share one approval contract. “Canonical asset found” was incorrectly treated as “identity approved.” |
| Cost & routing            | RED      | Tool routing happened before art-direction validation. Frontier reasoning, generation, and batch rendering were spent on an unapproved aesthetic, while no bounded exploration budget or stop gate prevented premature scale. |

TOP FIXES (ranked by blast radius)
1. Put a founder-controlled territory and typography gate before any logo, generated image, or product-family fanout.
2. Replace shared aesthetic defaults with a taste constitution plus an explicit cross-brand separation matrix and brand-specific visual grammar.
3. Separate maker, hygiene checks, independent critique, and human approval in the experiment ledger; no score may override a human rejection.

CALL: rethink — the production plumbing is salvageable, but the identity inputs, approval architecture, and taste evaluation must be rebuilt before another asset batch.
