# Provisional brand territories

These are deliberately different starting worlds, not finished identities. Each brand gets two territories so the founder can choose, reject, or synthesize before production. No territory is approved by its presence in this file.

## Cross-brand separation

| Brand | Emotional job | Default light | Composition | Primary evidence | Must never resemble |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FrankX | Trust a singular operator and his judgment | Daylight or warm neutral | Editorial, decisive, asymmetric | Real thinking, annotations, decisions, portraits, finished outcomes | A SaaS dashboard or trading cockpit |
| GenCreator | Feel invited and energized to make | Bright studio light | Expressive, modular, tactile | Works in progress, creator artifacts, tools, people | A productivity app or FrankX sub-brand |
| SIS | Trust the system under pressure | Clear institutional light with an optional night mode | Calm, rigorous, documentary | Receipts, governance, real operations, legible structure | Hacker sci-fi, Arcanea, or a command-center movie prop |
| Arcanea | Enter a world worth caring about | Luminous image-led light or deep mineral tone | Cinematic publishing, generous scale | Characters, places, objects, maps, lore, crafted editions | Purple fantasy software or an AI portal generator |

## FrankX

Strategic role: the personal authority brand. FrankX should make the intelligence, judgment, ambition, and humanity of the founder legible. It should not hide him behind a fictional machine interface.

### Territory A — Working intelligence (recommended for first proof)

- Thesis: show thinking becoming decisions and decisions becoming outcomes.
- Visual grammar: editorial pages, annotated documents, decisive crops, elegant rule work, useful diagrams, candid or art-directed portraiture, and generous margins.
- Material: warm white paper, deep ink, one lucid cobalt or vermilion signal, occasional metallic or screen texture when tied to a real artifact.
- Type behavior: sentence-case headlines with contrast between a literate display voice and a calm reading face; italics and punctuation carry more personality than uppercase tracking.
- Image behavior: real notebooks, plans, screens, talks, travel, training, making, and evidence of work.
- Memory device: a recurring margin annotation or decision mark derived from Frank's actual process, not a generic logo flourish.

### Territory B — The engineer's atelier

- Thesis: the future is hand-built through tools, prototypes, and disciplined craft.
- Visual grammar: workshop photography, exploded views, technical drawing used sparingly, large utilitarian typography, and material closeups.
- Material: chalk, aluminum, paper, rubber, oxide orange, blueprint ink, natural light.
- Risk: can become industrial-design cosplay unless every object is real.

Typeface trials, not commitments: ABC Arizona, Söhne, and GT America. Test with the actual name “FrankX,” long-form essays, product titles, numerals, and mobile navigation before selecting.

## GenCreator

Strategic role: the creator-facing brand for people turning ideas into owned, finished work. It needs visible human energy, generosity, and range.

### Territory A — Open studio (recommended for first proof)

- Thesis: creation is a place you can enter, not an operating system you must learn.
- Visual grammar: daylight, physical worktables, movable frames, taped studies, color swatches, rough and finished artifacts shown together.
- Material: paper, ink, textile, clay, screens, transparent film, bright but intelligent color.
- Type behavior: friendly, characterful grotesk or variable family with real rhythm; sentence case and relaxed spacing.
- Image behavior: creators in process, hands and tools where authentic, actual work, and imperfect intermediate states.
- Memory device: a flexible crop/window that reveals successive stages of one work.

### Territory B — New creative publishing

- Thesis: every release is a small cultural object.
- Visual grammar: artist books, independent magazines, surprising image/type pairings, strong sequencing, and collected editions.
- Material: uncoated stocks, saturated spot color, photocopy texture used with restraint, archival labels written in natural language.
- Risk: can become trend-led editorial collage without a stable product grammar.

Typeface trials, not commitments: GT Flexa, ABC Favorit, and GT Maru. Test expressive headlines, quiet onboarding copy, product names, and creator attribution.

## Starlight Intelligence Systems

Strategic role: the trusted substrate for agentic operations. It should feel credible enough for serious governance and clear enough for everyday operators.

### Territory A — Civic intelligence (recommended for first proof)

- Thesis: powerful systems should feel accountable, public-minded, and calm.
- Visual grammar: modern institutional publishing, clear diagrams, restrained data, documentary photography, and layouts that reveal evidence without theatricality.
- Material: warm white, deep navy or near-black ink, stone, linen, paper, a single oxidized red or safety yellow for intervention.
- Type behavior: highly legible humanist or grotesk text with a sober display voice; mono only inside actual code or aligned logs.
- Image behavior: real rooms, people, infrastructure, repositories, governance artifacts, and verified product capture.
- Memory device: a clear evidence bracket or provenance line that always connects a claim to its source.

### Territory B — Instrument grade

- Thesis: an operational system should feel as dependable as a calibrated instrument.
- Visual grammar: manuals, serial plates, diagnostic documents, large fields of whitespace, dense but orderly tables, and precise physical controls.
- Material: cream, black, enamel, brushed aluminum, safety red, stamped ink.
- Risk: can become retro-industrial or sterile if people and decisions disappear.

Typeface trials, not commitments: Söhne, ABC Diatype, and GT America. Test governance prose, dense tables, error states, numerals, and a printed report.

## Arcanea

Strategic role: a creative world and publishing universe. Arcanea should make imagined worlds feel culturally and emotionally real, not merely generated.

### Territory A — Living codex (recommended for first proof)

- Thesis: every world is a crafted edition that can keep growing.
- Visual grammar: book arts, illuminated margins reinterpreted with restraint, large full-bleed world imagery, maps, character plates, objects with provenance, and quiet reading pages.
- Material: parchment, rag paper, ink, mineral pigment, vellum, embossed metal, botanical and geological color.
- Type behavior: literary display and reading faces with contemporary editorial discipline; no all-caps fantasy titling.
- Image behavior: specific places, characters, tools, creatures, and artifacts with continuity; commissioned or generated imagery is treated as illustration, not UI wallpaper.
- Memory device: a living folio edge that records where a scene belongs in canon.

### Territory B — Sublime archive

- Thesis: encounter Arcanea like a museum exhibition of a world that exists.
- Visual grammar: monumental image scale, curatorial captions, dramatic silence, object photography, film stills, and exhibition-catalog sequencing.
- Material: ivory, deep lapis, carbon, vermilion, silver, translucent tissue.
- Risk: can become remote luxury unless creator participation remains visible.

Typeface trials, not commitments: Canela, GT Alpina, and ABC Arizona Flare. Test lore titles, long reading, map labels, dialogue, product covers, and small UI before selecting.
