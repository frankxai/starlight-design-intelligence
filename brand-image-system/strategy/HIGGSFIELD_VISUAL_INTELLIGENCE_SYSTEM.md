# Higgsfield Visual Intelligence Factory

## Outcome

Turn abundant image-generation access into a governed multi-brand learning system, not an asset-volume contest.

The factory connects four existing layers:

1. Brand Image System owns brand packs, surfaces, prompts, and the 30-point gate.
2. Higgsfield supplies web exploration and metered CLI/MCP generation.
3. Visual Intelligence OS owns provenance, rights state, curation, and usage history.
4. Multi-Brand Social OS owns brand routing, approval state, publication receipts, and performance learning.

The existing `starlight-agent-config/.loop/brand-image-factory` remains the portfolio-level loop. The Higgsfield model-bakeoff workflow in this repo is a provider sub-loop, not a second publishing system.

## The Core Distinction

| Lane | Use | Automation | Cost truth | Promotion rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Higgsfield web Unlimited | High-volume human exploration, style search, rough families | No unattended automation | The live web UI must show an Unlimited badge and applicable expiry | Export only selected candidates into the governed job folder |
| Higgsfield CLI/MCP | Reproducible jobs, model comparisons, provenance, controlled batches | Dry-run first and cost-capped | Live `higgsfield generate cost` plus account balance | No asset advances without inspection, rights state, and score |

An Unlimited badge on the website is not evidence that a CLI/MCP call is free. The CLI does not currently expose an Unlimited execution parameter. Every automated candidate is therefore treated as metered unless a future live preflight proves otherwise.

“GPT-2” in the Higgsfield catalog means `gpt_image_2`, an image model. It is not the legacy GPT-2 language model. Do not assume a generic GPT Image Unlimited offer includes GPT Image 2 without an account-specific badge.

## Portfolio Loop

```text
proven signal or source artifact
  -> brand and audience route
  -> surface-specific brief
  -> deterministic-first decision
  -> small model bake-off when generation is earned
  -> local outputs + cost receipt + provenance
  -> independent 30-point visual and rights review
  -> one promoted master + deterministic overlays/crops
  -> brand packet at approval_pending
  -> human publish decision
  -> publication receipt + performance result
  -> weekly model/brand learning update
```

Exact claims, labels, citations, prices, metrics, diagrams, UI, and calls to action are always rendered deterministically after the source-frame selection. Generated pixels may supply a photographic, illustrative, cinematic, or material base; they are not the source of truth for public copy.

## Model-Selection Intelligence

Do not declare one universal winner. Store selection evidence at the intersection of:

- brand unit;
- surface and crop;
- visual role;
- model and settings;
- prompt family;
- predicted and observed cost;
- score by the six five-point dimensions;
- rejection reason;
- rights and approval state;
- downstream performance after human publication.

The useful metric is not generations per day. It is approved, reusable masters per reviewed candidate, with full evidence and a low rejection cost.

### Default bake-off

- Three models, one candidate each.
- One shared outcome contract and prompt family.
- One 1K draft per model.
- Five-credit hard ceiling for the v1 runner unless an operator explicitly changes the environment ceiling.
- One targeted iteration only when the remaining cap covers it.
- Stop after a winner, not after a quota target.

The first controlled family uses Nano Banana 2 (`nano_banana_flash`), FLUX.2 (`flux_2`), and Seedream 5 Lite (`seedream_v5_lite`). Live cost preflight supersedes all recorded hints.

## Brand Routing Principles

The machine-readable routes live in `runtime/model-router.v1.json`.

| Brand unit | Evidence-first source | Generated-media role | Special gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| FrankX | Real product, operating artifact, deterministic diagram | Executive editorial source frames and campaign plates | No fake command-center UI or artificial founder likeness |
| GenCreator / Creator Systems | Real UI, cohort artifacts, exact stack maps | Reference-faithful creator scenes and modular backgrounds | Product proof must remain visible and exact |
| Arcanea | Canon, world bible, approved character refs | Cinematic worlds, original characters, motion source frames | Canon and IP review before promotion |
| Starlight | Protocol docs, release receipts, exact system maps | Restrained operational-editorial support imagery | Public/private boundary and claim review |
| AI Architect / CoE | Architecture proof, governance diagrams, implementation evidence | Non-factual enterprise editorial plates | Enterprise claims require sources |
| Reality Architect | Concrete methods, practices, timelines | Grounded transformation/editorial scenes | No vague mystical or outcome-guarantee framing |
| Anime Legends | Original canon and reserved character sheets | Character/world exploration and motion plates | Original-IP and likeness review |

VibeClubs exists in the older image-system mirror but is not one of the ten current publishing units. It stays `decision_required` until a human brand-identity and account-activation decision promotes it.

## Automation Maturity

### Stage 0 — current proof

- Manual job selection.
- Runner validates contract, checks account, preflights each candidate, and enforces the cap.
- Outputs and portable VIS sidecars are created locally.
- Independent QA selects or rejects.
- Downstream packet stops at `approval_pending`.

### Stage 1 — after three clean manual cycles

- Bind the workflow to the existing Brand Image Factory queue.
- Automate contact-sheet and evidence-packet assembly.
- Keep execution approval per batch.
- Keep publication human-only.

### Stage 2 — after stable economics and rejection data

- Add a bounded Codex automation for brief preparation and review reminders.
- Do not schedule generation merely because web generation is abundant.
- Introduce n8n or Make only if retries, external webhooks, or cross-service approvals cannot be handled cleanly by the native queue and Codex automation.

### Stage 3 — performance learning

- Join publication receipts to model/brand/surface evidence.
- Update routing weekly from approved-master rate, reuse rate, review time, and downstream performance.
- Preserve anti-Goodhart language: metrics are descriptive snapshots, not volume targets.

## Required Gates

- Machine: workload-specific PP preflight is `allow` or `bounded`.
- Cost: every candidate has a successful live preflight and the total is inside the job cap.
- Balance: predicted post-run balance is above the job guardrail.
- Concurrency: the recent transaction quiet window contains no other account spend.
- Source: brief, brand pack, surface, audience, and visual role are explicit.
- Visual: actual exports inspected; 26/30 minimum, 28/30 for flagship masters.
- Provenance: prompt, model, settings, local path, SHA-256, agent, and job are recorded.
- Rights: unknown or blocked rights cannot be public-ready.
- Text: public text and factual diagrams are deterministic.
- Approval: scheduling and publishing require named human approval.
- Learning: rejected candidates retain a reason; selected assets retain the comparison receipt.

## Thirty-Day Operating Cadence

- Week 1: one controlled prompt family each for FrankX, GenCreator, Arcanea, Starlight, and Anime Legends.
- Week 2: approve one reusable visual family per priority brand; document reject patterns.
- Week 3: derive surface-native crops and deterministic overlay templates from approved masters.
- Week 4: assemble a 30-day approval reserve, then review model economics and brand fit.

Tier 2 and Tier 3 units receive generated media only when a proven content signal and distinct audience justify the review cost. The system optimizes for recognizable brand memory and reusable masters, not equal generation allocation.

## Success Measures

- Approved-master rate per model, brand, and surface.
- Median review minutes per promoted master.
- Credits per promoted master in the metered lane.
- Reuse count across valid crops and surfaces.
- Rejection reasons by prompt family and model.
- Provenance and rights completeness.
- Percentage of packets that reach `approval_pending` without gate exceptions.
- Post-publication saves, qualified clicks, replies, and conversions, interpreted by brand mission rather than one portfolio-wide vanity score.

## Stop Conditions

Stop the run when the machine preflight is `hold`, the CLI cannot authenticate, account or cost data is unavailable, predicted spend exceeds the cap, post-run balance crosses the guardrail, a candidate lacks a downloadable result, provenance cannot be written, or the only path forward requires a public, identity, legal, account, or recurring-automation decision.
