# Ana infographic brief

**Title:** One intelligence, many doorways.

**Audience:** Ana first; then a future ally, collaborator, or thoughtful client.

**Job:** Make the system legible in one glance without turning a reflective practice into a software dashboard.

**Visual thesis:** A warm central voice holds six practical pathways. The system grows by cycling approved work through sense, memory, composition, hosting, delivery, and compounding.

**Palette:** Ana Brand OS ivory `#FBF6EC`, espresso `#2E1E14`, gold `#C9A961`, rose clay `#C28F7C`, midnight plum `#2D1B33`; restrained emerald `#1F5F4A` for the freedom signal.

**Composition:** Deterministic SVG carries all text and relationships. Higgsfield Recraft V4.1 supplies an abstract tactile pathway plate with no words, so the visual remains inspirational while exact labels stay accessible and editable.

**Human and safety posture:** Ana remains the author and decision-maker. Client material is private by default. Nothing publishes without Ana's sign-off. `cecilia.chat` is not represented as a public runtime.

**Next possible frames:** offer-ladder card, bilingual delivery-loop carousel, private companion architecture, and a twelve-month compounding roadmap.
