## Decision

The research, brief, deterministic `ana-possibility-map.svg`, and generated pathway plate are worth preserving in `starlight-design-intelligence` as source material. The package is not production-ready.

`ana-finished-infographic.webp` fails the generated-text gate. Its typography contains malformed or ambiguous words, its exact copy is not backed by an editable overlay, and it has no dedicated prompt/provenance receipt. The file name is historical; it is **rejected as a final export**.

`ana-possibility-map.svg` is the correct restart point because its text and relationships are deterministic, accessible, and editable. It still needs a deliberate composition pass, verified desktop/mobile or social crops, factual-owner review, and a new inspected export before release.

`higgsfield-vision-plate.svg` and its WebP derivative are Tier-D decorative source material for this surface, not the primary infographic asset. The associated receipt records `rightsStatus: needs-review`; therefore they must not ship until rights are cleared.
