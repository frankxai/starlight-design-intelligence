# Multi-Brand Visual Strategy: Next 90 Days

This strategy keeps the image loop useful instead of letting it become a folder of disconnected experiments.

## Month 1: Foundation And Benchmark

Goal: prove the system works across brands, channels, and tools.

- Finish central brand packs for GenCreator, AnimeLegends, Reality Architect, AI CoE, Agentic Income, Research Intelligence, and Tooling/OSS.
- Run the image-tool benchmark from `tool-benchmark-plan.md`.
- Create one approved source image family per priority brand:
  - FrankX Demand.
  - Starlight Substrate.
  - Arcanea Product and IP.
  - Creator Systems / GenCreator.
  - Anime Legends / Media IP.
- Build deterministic overlay templates for LinkedIn, X, Instagram feed, Instagram story/reel, YouTube thumbnail, TikTok, and website OG.
- Create prompt logs and evidence manifests for every approved family.

Exit gate:

- 5 brand families approved at 26/30+.
- Tool benchmark results recorded.
- No generated visual accepted without output inspection.

## Month 2: Production Cadence

Goal: turn approved visual families into repeatable publishing assets.

- Weekly content-to-visual sprint from `#social-command`.
- For each approved campaign, export:
  - 1:1 square.
  - 4:5 feed crop when useful.
  - 9:16 story/reel/shorts crop.
  - 16:9 or 1.91:1 website/OG/YouTube crop.
- Pair each asset family with a caption set and channel notes.
- Record which prompts/tools produce the strongest brand fit and lowest artifact risk.
- Use product capture and code-rendered overlays for proof-heavy FrankX, Starlight, AI CoE, and Tooling surfaces.

Exit gate:

- 4 weeks of usable channel-ready asset packets.
- Rejected/weak prompts logged with reasons.
- Reusable overlay templates exist for major channels.

## Month 3: Learning And Portfolio Strategy

Goal: improve strategy with evidence, not taste debates.

- Compare performance by brand, channel, format, and visual family.
- Identify winning first reads:
  - Proof.
  - Mechanism.
  - World.
  - Outcome.
  - Contrarian claim.
- Tighten each brand's visual lane based on actual engagement and conversion signals.
- Decide which incubator lanes earn a central brand pack.
- Archive underperforming prompt families and route the strong ones into future site, deck, and campaign systems.

Exit gate:

- Monthly visual review with examples, scores, and performance notes.
- Updated brand packs and prompt rules.
- Clear next-month production queue by brand operating unit.
