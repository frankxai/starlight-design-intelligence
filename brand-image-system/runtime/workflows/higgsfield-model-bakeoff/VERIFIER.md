# Verifier

Inspect the actual exported files, not prompts or thumbnails.

Score each candidate from 0-5 on:

1. First read and hierarchy.
2. Brand fit and ownability.
3. Craft, composition, lighting, spacing, and crop.
4. Accessibility, contrast, and crop resilience.
5. Accuracy, provenance, and absence of artifacts.
6. Usefulness for the intended surface.

Record:

- total score out of 30;
- pass, iterate, restart, or blocked;
- concrete strengths;
- concrete defects;
- rights status;
- crop risk;
- reason for selecting or rejecting the candidate.

Promotion requires 26/30; flagship use requires 28/30. A no-winner decision is correct when no candidate clears the gate. The verifier may recommend one targeted iteration but cannot raise the cost cap, publish, or change brand identity.
