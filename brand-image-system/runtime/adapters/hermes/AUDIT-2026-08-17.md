## Why the result failed

1. The reasoning model, source-image model, and final renderer were conflated in the handoff.
2. Source prompts explicitly banned readable text and logos, but the final renderer did not reintroduce canonical brand assets with enough craft.
3. The same sparse template was forced onto books, sprints, kits, SaaS, courses, PDFs, and internal holds.
4. Existing logos were approximated as typed labels instead of loaded as assets.
5. The product-object photographs communicated atmosphere rather than product mechanism or proof.
6. The generated sources and deterministic covers were separate production systems; most source images did not materially inform the final covers.
7. No useful benchmark ledger captured prompt version, model receipt, form fit, crop inspection, or improvement delta.

## Replacement contract

- Classify product form and release state before art direction.
- Resolve product truth and canonical brand assets before prompting.
- Prefer real product/proof for live surfaces.
- Use premium generation only for illustration, atmosphere, or physical-artifact source layers.
- Render logos, titles, claims, status, diagrams, and UI deterministically.
- Produce editable master plus inspected channel crops.
- Track every candidate against the six-part 30-point gate.
- Treat `score30 < 22` as restart, `22–27` as iterate, and `28–30` as ship candidate pending human approval.

The source of truth for the overhaul is the `2026-08-17-product-cover-overhaul` experiment package and the `product-cover` / `product-launch-kit` workflow packs.
