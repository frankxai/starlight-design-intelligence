# Marketing Asset Portfolio by Product Form

One cover is not a launch system. Each form needs a different proof object and a different minimum asset family.

| Product form | Cover mechanism | Proof asset | Minimum validation family | Launch expansion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Book | editorial jacket with author/title hierarchy | sample spread or real manuscript page | portrait cover, thumbnail, sample spread, OG | print jacket, retailer crops, quote cards, author social |
| PDF / guide | document cover with information architecture | real interior page | cover, page preview, OG, download card | landing hero, email header, 3–5 insight cards |
| Workbook | tactile working surface and structured fields | real exercise page | cover, interior worksheet, completion artifact | lesson cards, testimonial/proof frame after use |
| Course / academy | curriculum path and learner outcome | real module or lesson capture | course card, curriculum map, instructor/proof frame | cohort social, lesson trailers, completion badge |
| Sprint / diagnostic | timeboxed decision instrument | real output artifact and criteria | offer cover, process strip, deliverable stack, scope-page OG | application asset, email header, decision memo preview |
| Kit / pack | visible contents and transformation mechanism | real files/components or exact diagram | cover, contents card, mechanism infographic, delivery preview | use-case cards, onboarding frames, buyer portal assets |
| SaaS / platform | real interface and outcome state | verified product capture | product card, desktop/mobile proof, feature mechanism | hero, demo stills, comparison, changelog/social |
| Implementation / high touch | before → intervention → governed result | anonymized delivery artifact | offer cover, scope map, deliverables, application asset | case study, qualification deck, private scope page |
| Community / retainer | operating cadence and access model | real calendar, ritual, or artifact flow | program card, cadence map, who-it-is-for card | cohort announcement, member proof, event assets |
| Internal hold | no public marketing cover | source/rights/claims resolution | internal registry tile only | none until human gate clears |

## Shared files per approved product

Every approved product should eventually have:

- editable master (`HTML/CSS`, `SVG`, `Figma`, or `Canva`),
- transparent canonical logo assets and font receipts,
- portrait cover (`1080×1440`),
- product/social portrait (`1080×1350`),
- square (`1080×1080`),
- open graph (`1200×630`),
- website desktop hero (`1920×1080`) and mobile hero (`1080×1920`) when a page exists,
- one real proof frame,
- prompt/model/source log,
- crop inspection and 30-point QA evidence.

Generate the launch expansion only after the product release state and message are approved. A queued product needs an internal concept and validation asset, not a full public campaign.
